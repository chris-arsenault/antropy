# Scalar illumination correction: bounded evidence

September 21, 2026. Registration and design:
[one illumination field](../../design/light-ecology.md#scalar-correction-verification-registration).
Physical v34 uses one scalar for reaction work, sensing and display. No shelter, emission,
dormancy, changed binding or new default period was implemented.

## Short ordinary-World probes

[Assays](assays.json) retain zero-tick budgets, phase samples, evaluator costs, full configurations
and bounded sampled observations. [Ledger records and native binary hash](assay-ledger.json)
identify rows 4262–4269. The existing supplied fixtures freeze learning and mutation, source
release/weathering and movement. Four uniform controls survive 300 ticks. The two bright cases
survive 300 ticks; dim role1 and role2 die at 114 and 67. Neither horizon nor seed was extended.
These results support magnitude-dependent metabolic pressure, not evolved activity schedules.

Both roles' initial reaction work rises with light. At initial sampled light .3741/1/1.6259,
role1 obtains .02030/.14373/.26717 work per physiology interval, and role2 obtains
.00955/.13931/.26906. Each initially owes .01219 maintenance per interval, before other costs.
No relative-role survival reversal is required. All sampled absolute accounting residuals
are below 6.41e-12.

## Cost and startup

[Before capacity](capacity-before.json), rows 4259–4261, and
[after capacity](capacity-after.json), rows 4270–4272, retain binary hashes and configs.
They use 10 warmup plus 100 measured ticks, 60-second caps and the shared fixed/growing workloads.
Throughput is 62.54→62.50, 29.74→29.66 and 19.32→19.52 ticks/s. No material regression is resolved
by these single short samples; mature-state scalability remains untested here.

[Ordinary startup](startup.json), row 4273, reaches 3,000 ticks in 17.86 seconds with 187 cells.
The [manifest](startup-manifest.json) records config, binary/source provenance and wall cap.
There were 240 divisions and 101 deaths. The horizon is insufficient to assess a full light
period or evolved nocturnal activity. No long campaign followed.

Full binaries, checkpoints and traces remain local under
`frontend/harness/artifacts/illumination-scalar-v34/`. Reports are copied here without changing
their content. The v33 historical run was neither resumed nor migrated.
