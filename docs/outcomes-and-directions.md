# Outcomes and open directions

**Status:** Current reference — observed outcomes, negative results and open research directions; companion to the [white paper](white-paper.md).

Biotropy runs cells with local heritable RNNs in an accounted 256-chemical world; the
[white paper](white-paper.md) owns its question, mathematics and rationale. This appendix uses
four evidence levels: an implemented mechanism exists; a constructed opportunity shows a link in a
handcrafted probe; observed inherited variation appears in a long run; demonstrated evolved
exploitation shows an inherited choice repaying its cost. These are distinct claims, and none
substitutes for another ([evidence levels](design/experimentation.md#experiments-evidence-levels),
[principles](principles.md)). Figures below are quoted from the linked records, with their original
versions and denominators.

## What has emerged

### Pre-chemistry world (September 11–13): coexistence passes withdrawn

The TypeScript A/B-food world's reported coexistence passes were withdrawn after an
[audit](analysis-correction.md) found descendant growth confused with share growth. In the
seven-mechanism campaign, 3 of 24 rare-start pairs increased share in both directions at the
12,000-tick endpoint, not the reported 16; one of the three moved +0.072 percentage points.
Inherited diet differences and opposite-band occupancy in the thick-medium study remain a
proof point for local resource selection, with several world parameters changed together.

### v31, September 19: 200,000 ticks

The [integrated review](integrated-200k-review.md) ran seed 27 to its horizon: 129 cells at the
end, maximum living generation 335, sampled peak 1,012 cells at 48k. A 92k–100k starvation
collapse took population from 781 to 91; one cell alive at 100k is ancestor to all 129 final cells.

- Heritable body differentiation: at 200k, 26/129 cells above mass 10 held 79.9% of living
  biomass, with a median inherited newborn core target of 6.170 versus 0.432 for the rest. They
  produced intermediates 98 and 114 internally rather than importing them.
- Evolved optical use: optical inputs began with zero founder connections; at 150k, removing
  them changed an action above 0.01 in 287/361 cells. Sun-seeking and its benefit were not shown.
- Feedstock dependence: 97.71–98.43% of final-10k imports were chemicals 0 or 136. Reciprocal
  conversion was 1.47% of accepted flow. Majority-nonfeed candidates (4/113 at 200k) appeared, but
  the eleven identified before 200k left no living descendants.
- Resource sites dispersed despite binding: source nearest-neighbour median rose from 5.07 to 21.38.

### v32, September 20–21: user checkpoint at 83,980 ticks

The read-only [habitat review](material-habitats-review.md) found 3,774 cells, maximum living
generation 244 and three principal regions of 2,669, 754 and 337 cells with different common
enzyme routes (for example 37↔69 in the largest, 81→97 and 138→106 in the eastern region).
2,803/3,774 cells (74.27%) obtained a majority of recorded imports outside chemicals 0/136. Net
consumption remained dominated by 0 (198.67) and 136 (26.15), and mean reservoir renewal stayed
87.193% initial 0/136. All cells descended from founder 17; 97.5% were born after 80k. Chart
samples begin at 60,200, so the early transition is unrecorded.

### v33, September 20: 180,000 ticks, stopped from a planned 200,000

The user stopped the run for cost; the [180k analysis](cellular-200k-review.md) and
[connected review](cellular-180k-connections.md) use the validated 180k checkpoint.

- 6,975 cells, maximum generation 233, after 115,213 divisions and 108,286 starvation deaths.
  Three branches from cells alive at 40k: 5,691 northern, 1,109 southern, 175 peripheral.
- Divergent processing: north consumed mainly 119/120/135/136, south 80/96/112/128; their
  processing-overlap score fell from 0.610 at 100k to 0.066 at 180k while uptake overlap stayed 51.6%.
- Private recycling dominates the large colonies: median self-matched consumption 98.6% north,
  98.3% south, 0.1% periphery. Direct neighbour transfer supplied 1.95% of cumulative uptake.
- Collective habitat effect: omitting all body signals cut frozen work-rate opportunity by 69.3%;
  omitting each cell's own body cut it by 0.64%. This is not evidence of evolved cooperation.
- Energy crises with retained material: a southern family fell to four cells at 114k, then
  recovered to 1,109 through one surviving branch.
- 4,583/6,617 eligible importers (69.3%) obtained most imports outside 0/136; seed-ID share of
  sampled source output fell from 96.1% (0–40k) to 73.2% (170k–180k).
- Conditional regulation stayed weak: 39 cells showed substantial selective enzyme activity, and
  63.5% of net reaction work in 170k–180k overflowed energy capacity.

### v40–v41, September 23: server dispersion and class-specific coupling

In the continuing v40 server world, reservoir mean nearest-neighbour distance rose
13.9 → 16.6 → 19.8 → 20.2 units at 98k/217k/412k/725k, against about 20.1 for a random layout;
cell dense-core share fell from 19% to zero ([decision record](design/decisions-and-evidence.md#class-specific-material-coupling)).
V41 coupled reservoirs and cells separately to the shared chemical response. In a paired 150k
seed-27 run ([coupling plan](plans/archive/MATERIAL-COUPLING-PLAN.md)), v41 retained 76 groups
with largest 24 at 150k versus v40's 123 with largest 10, ended at 2,937 cells versus 475, and
reached 1.8% superorganism share at 30k and none later. The live v41 server world still dispersed
its reservoirs to nearly random by tick 698k. After the reservoir-exposure change, a run held
5,799 cells at 60k ticks (1,112 before), 92% in dense cores and 0.1% isolated
([composed laws](design/chemistry/composed-runtime.md)); its longer behaviour was left to the server.

### v42, September 25: young terrain world

A three-minute spectator watch of the reset v42 server world, ticks 40,852–47,177, saw population
move from 1,366 to 1,582, the largest proximity group hold 601/1,582 cells (38%) and contact rise
from 30.3% to 40.9% of cells ([placement study](cluster-placement-study.md#running-server)).
This is clustering in a young world, not a causal terrain measurement.

### v43, September 28: live dispersal window

A read-only analysis of ticks 606,669–612,671 ([dispersal evidence](evidence/dispersal-v43/README.md))
recorded 360 departures from 358 cells: 278 ended in starvation, 14 returned, 68 remained
underway, and none completed an observed crossing to another reservoir system. Among 36 cells
already away when capture began, one travelled 83.45 units and left 11 living descendants in an
existing colony; another travelled 54.42 units to an unoccupied system and died without dividing.
The 32 systems had a median nearest surface gap of 53.75 units. These findings motivated v44.

### v44–v46: no long-run evidence recorded

V44 (outlying reservoirs, neural row budget), v45 (birth orientation, aging) and v46 (fractal
terrain, local seasons) have bounded mechanism checks only. The v44 record states that no
live-world behaviour has been measured under it; no v45 or v46 long-run review exists.

The [work order](design/README.md#design-goal-and-present-evidence) summarizes the standing
position: differentiated bodies, inherited processing and repeated die-off/recovery are observed;
feedstock dependence and weak public exchange remain.

## Constructed opportunities and negative results

- **Paid photoreception, v31** ([record](photoreception.md)). Opposite neural connections produced
  opposite turns; unbuilt and uniform-light controls did not. All four finite-reserve probes died
  before 300 ticks (87–99) without importing material. Sensing and steering, not a benefit.
- **Public-food consumer, v32** ([decision record](design/decisions-and-evidence.md#environmental-operators-and-habitat-formation)).
  Captured work 0.05609 did not cover repair 0.17348; the consumer died at 150 ticks versus 182
  for its control. No viable public-food niche has been demonstrated.
- **Chemical shelter, v14** (same record; [results](design/chemistry/environmental-results.md)). Expensive ID15 shelter lowered exposure but lost
  construction and survival; cheaper ID245 gave a small construction return without a survival
  advantage. In the overnight study seed 27 went extinct in both arms; the selected genotype's
  shelter-specific advantage failed.
- **Light ecology, v42** ([checks](light-ecology-results.md)). Terrain-response probes turned toward
  or away from light and exhausted their reserves. Baseline builders' deposits fell below the
  public-material cutoff; a four-fold builder accumulated film but did not outlive its idle
  control. Emission produced a small paid turn in an off-axis receiver; recovered optical work was
  far below emitter expenditure. No whole-cell shelter or lamp payback.
- **Cluster placement, v42** ([study](cluster-placement-study.md)). Close placement of two sampled
  live genotypes left 50 and 47 cells at 1,500 ticks; spread placement went extinct at tick 77.
  The [follow-up](cluster-bottleneck-study.md) found the fixture had overfilled storage, so the
  result does not show that saved cells cannot live alone.
- **Cluster bottleneck, v42** ([study](cluster-bottleneck-study.md)). Removing adhesion raised
  one-step travel 16.5% but left populations clumped. In the mature world, removing neighbour
  bodies kept about 84% of net reaction income, against 14% in the small fixture. All four
  actual-state transplant populations went extinct. The production public-chemistry cutoff omitted
  41.1% of permitted conversion in one frozen interval; the resulting participation recommendation
  was withdrawn when the user excluded chemical conversion from that task.
- **Movement opportunity, v42** ([study](movement-opportunity-study.md)). Around a finite 96-material
  patch, eight swimmers against eight residents imported 89.27/91.27 versus 36.74/33.58 material
  and left 11/11 versus 3/4 living descendants at 1,500 ticks. Ordinary movement can repay motor
  cost for a temporary patch; this is an authored policy, not evolved dispersal.
- **Ecological incentives, v42** ([final results](plans/archive/ECOLOGICAL-INCENTIVES-PLAN.md#final-results-and-delivery)).
  A lit isolated cell survived 300 ticks while its dark control died at tick 59, but free material
  fell from 0.8 to about 0.00517. Receptor steering cut harmful exposure 37.2% but ended with lower
  energy. With dilute material, light built 2.39 times the shade body. Crowded overlaps fell from
  42 to zero. No evolved dispersal is claimed ([work order](design/README.md#design-ecological-incentives)).
- **Dispersal changes, v44** ([plan](plans/archive/DISPERSAL-OPPORTUNITIES-PLAN.md)). Outlying placement
  cut the median surface gap from 16.58 to 13.97 units with matched supply. The row budget removed
  saturated turning (11 to zero of 52) but lowered median turning response from 0.113 to 0.0265
  and left 37/52 energy-insensitive swimmers; it does not supply missing conditional policies.
- **Mature-world performance** ([decision record](design/decisions-and-evidence.md#mature-world-performance-investigation)).
  The single-threaded plan to reach 120 ticks/s on the 6,975-cell 180k world was canceled near
  14 ticks/s; its local-execution caches regressed to 5.6–10.1 ticks/s. The regional multicore
  runtime replaced that structure, and no matched mature-world measurement has followed.

## What has not been shown

- An ecosystem independent of original feedstock: net material flow and renewal remained
  dominated by chemicals 0/136 in v31, v32 and v33.
- A food web built on exchanged products: private recycling carries the large v33 colonies, and
  direct neighbour transfer stays near 2% of uptake.
- Evolved cooperation, obligate multicellularity or profitable deliberate injury.
- Communication: pre-chemistry neutral-signal secretion stayed low, and utterances are unimplemented.
- A profitable shelter, lamp or public-food strategy in any probe.
- Evolved dispersal or colonization: no observed v43 crossing founded a new colony.
- Useful directional light navigation, beyond neural sensitivity to optical inputs.
- Substantial conditional metabolic switching or selective enzyme scheduling.
- Indefinite diversity or persistent coexistence of distinct strategies; the old 20- and
  200-generation gates were never met.
- Any ecological effect of v44–v46 mechanisms over long horizons.
- Uninterrupted days-long operation ([continuing observation](continuing-observation.md)).

## Open directions

The [design directions registry](design/directions.md) lists every direction with its status;
the [backlog](backlog.md) holds the unimplemented work list.

### Deferred proposals

- **Directional cell utterances** — paid byte broadcasts with coarse 16-sector listener-relative
  bearing, no material or work delivery. Deferred, specified September 24
  ([specification](design/cell-utterances.md), [backlog](backlog.md#backlog-cell-utterances)).
- **Strategic controller** — a slow heritable Elman RNN whose outputs become reflex-RNN inputs and
  a learning gain; it issues no actions. Deferred, specified September 30, expected after
  utterances ([specification](design/strategic-controller.md), [backlog](backlog.md#backlog-strategic-controller)).

### Unselected extensions

- **Terrain dynamics** — slowly changing geography, tectonics, catastrophic topology changes and
  live physical switches beyond static v46 terrain. Unselected September 30
  ([backlog](backlog.md#backlog-terrain-refinement), [persistent geography](design/persistent-geography.md)).
- **Rotational or directional climate** — never selected; needs a measured limitation of the
  present shared fields ([backlog](backlog.md#backlog-conditional-extensions)).
- **Bonded attachment** — persistent links with attachment, detachment, forces and costs beyond
  v41 contact adhesion. Unselected ([decision](design/decisions-and-evidence.md#contact-adhesion-and-bonded-attachment)).
- **Multiple-substrate reactions, evolvable mutation machinery and neural topology** — unselected
  pending a demonstrated representation or chemistry limitation ([backlog](backlog.md#backlog-conditional-extensions)).
- **Dormancy and additional life stages** — unselected; needs an opportunity current physiology
  cannot express (same backlog section).
- **Outcrossing, multi-parent ancestry and developmental reaction norms** — unselected; each lacks
  a lifecycle or payment decision (same backlog section).
- **Light ecology questions** — ecological payoff of shelter and emission, nocturnal endurance
  ([backlog](backlog.md#backlog-light-ecology)).
- **Interaction research** — evolved cooperation, direct consumption of living structure and
  larger reaction arity; no campaign required ([backlog](backlog.md#backlog-cell-interactions)).
- **Future performance work** — 16/32-core scaling, larger-world and persistence costs, under the
  structural-first rule. Unselected September 30 ([backlog](backlog.md#backlog-multicore-scaling)).
- **Runtime and observation limits** — browser termination and quota behaviour untested; the
  measured-flow observer still needs route-key correction ([backlog](backlog.md#backlog-runtime-and-observation-limits)).

### Open hypotheses

From the [hypothesis log](hypothesis-log.md):

- **H1, mutation magnitude** — whether mutation reaches substantially different machinery.
  Heavy-tail law implemented; waste feeding unestablished.
- **H2, universal energy yield** — whether shared yield per transition drives convergence on the
  same processing. Open; no heritable efficiency gene exists.
- **H3, history-dependent escape from founder waste** — supported for the measured 20k histories
  by the [#186 causal study](186-symmetry-causality.md); general reliability unresolved.
- **H4, local cell interactions** — crowded access, paid exchange and injured-neighbour access are
  implemented in v33; whether a strategy using them repays its costs is untested.
