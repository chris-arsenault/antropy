## Outcome and scope

Determine which installed mechanisms ordinary cells express, what those actions accomplish,
what repays their costs, and which consequences survive individual replacement. Use common
physical checkpoints and explicit interventions to separate those questions. Cover the
[direction registry](../design/directions.md), including foundations and operational features,
and account explicitly for proposals that have no implementation to toggle.

The first recommendation is to compare activity reinforcement, optical investment and paid
communication in the current mature world, followed by local recovery and strategic memory.
Recent observation motivates these comparisons: allocation is expressed, emission has little
energetic recapture, and extensive replacement makes total population a weak discriminator.
Those observations select questions; they do not establish the benefit or harm of a feature.

This is an analysis protocol, not a new release gate or an instruction to redesign every
negative feature. Planning authorizes this document, not live interventions, fresh-world
restarts or the campaign below. No experiments, controls, deployment or runtime edits occur
while authoring it. Execution should cover the complete matrix in bounded batches and report
untestable claims explicitly, rather than silently dropping them or stretching failed runs.

## Questions and estimands

Give every scenario four separate conclusions where the observations permit them:

| Claim | Required evidence | Insufficient proxy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanism works | The intended physical/state update and its accounts respond to a declared intervention | A flag exists or an inspector field changes |
| Ordinary cells express it | Actual paid activity, accepted effects and relevant private state in an unmodified population | Installed capability or possible-route count |
| Expression has a causal return | Common-checkpoint treatment changes the predicted cue → action → accepted consequence → payer return chain | More activity, biomass or population alone |
| Inherited ecological consequence | Expressed differences and their returns continue in descendants through relevant replacement and supply transitions | Genotype count, ancestry colors or one survivor snapshot |

The primary comparison is the treatment-minus-reference change over the same declared model-time
window. Report payer and recipient separately, plus neighborhood and world effects. A saved
world is a paired experimental unit; its thousands of cells are not thousands of independent
replicates. Cells and reservoir neighborhoods remain useful strata for locating mechanisms.

Use actual accepted import/export, consumption/production, usable capture, operating expenses,
energy reserves, injury/repair, funded biomass growth and divisions/deaths. Keep paid work,
material quantities and event counts in their own units. For the registered gross operating remainder, subtract
maintenance, motors, transport, repair, learning, growth work, cover work, speech work and
emission from positive captured work, then division work. This excludes uphill reaction spending
and overflow/death heat and is not a net return. The source checks below derive uphill spending
from the usable-reserve balance; retain actual reserve and material changes for lifecycle judgments.
Reconcile against the ordinary ledger rather than inventing a new score. Do not
subtract reaction heat a second time from usable capture, count optical recycling as additional
external work, or add the reflex/strategic learning subaccounts to their already-inclusive total.

Gross chemical histories include private cycles, passive exchange and reimports. Different
dominant chemical labels do not prove cross-feeding. Source stock, release, corpse recovery and
external imports are separate accounts. Nonseed consumption does not establish independence
from the external supply that generated it. Count fission divisions separately from daughter
birth records and natural deaths; report the demographic closure under the selected lifecycle.

Use cumulative world accounts for population intervals and follow an initially declared focal
cohort through births and deaths for payer/recipient returns. Summing only current survivors'
lifetime flows creates survivorship bias. The server's detailed ending records are sampled
above 64 cells; they cannot supply complete cohort accounts. Use bounded native study tracing
where those accounts are essential, or label the per-cell conclusion incomplete. Compare
initial/final reserves, inventory and bound material as well as operating margins: growth or
reproduction can spend usable work without reducing the cell's total retained resources.

anchors by observable opportunities before looking at treatment outcomes:

### Run one comparison from one state

1. Capture status, effective config/revision, diagnostics, checkpoint index and ecology with
   `server-review`. Identify format, checkpoint SHA256, kernel revision, seed, tick, model time,
   random-state provenance and observer windows. A shared seed is not a shared checkpoint.
2. For a server comparison, pause and store a durable manual return point S. Confirm it exists,
   is compatible and has no unresolved pending patch. Record original running state and speed.
   The server has eight manual slots and a byte budget: reuse anchors; never delete unrelated
   points automatically. Download S for local analysis when needed.
3. Restore S paused before **every** arm, including the reference. Loading increments generation;
   refresh generation and revision. Do not chain A → B without rewind, or use a later baseline.
4. Apply the arm's complete declared patch atomically. Confirm effective settings and any pending
   patch at the completed physiology boundary. A paused mid-interval patch remains pending;
   ordinary stepping settles the old interval. Start treatment measurements only at the actual
   application boundary and record that tick.
5. Match intervention handling in the reference. `configure` invalidates controller caches and
   refreshes sensing even for an unchanged value. Submit an explicit unchanged-value patch through
   `/api/control` for the reference; `native_run.py` skips unchanged settings and cannot supply
   this sham by itself. Retain the reference's actual revision and boundary too.
6. Resume, collect the defined observations, then pause at the declared window or event limit.
   Save each arm's captures locally **before** rewind. Measure cumulative-ledger differences;
   resident windows crossing the application boundary contain mixed treatment and are excluded
   or explicitly labeled. Repeated resident windows must not be summed as independent flows.
7. Compare equivalent model-time intervals; retain actual endpoints. Wall-clock duration and
   maximum-speed throughput are not biological exposure. Normal polling can overshoot a boundary:
   report overshoot, compare common coverage, and do not add an exact-step service to remove it.
8. Restore the intended continuation point and original config, running state and speed. The
   default server disposition is S with ordinary features enabled, not the winning experimental
   branch. Explain the discarded experimental time. Confirm successful persistence and leave
   the populated continuing world running when it was running before the comparison.

Prefer independent local restores of downloaded S for many arms and dense traces so the public

## Scenario matrix

Each scenario starts with zero-tick budgets and existing evidence. Diagnostic edits below are
explicit counterfactuals on isolated copies or handcrafted cells; they are not ordinary evolution,
available live toggles or permission to change the production law. Keep the cell's material,
energy, private experience and surroundings matched except for the declared intervention.

### A. Activity reinforcement, conditional allocation and paid reflex learning

**Question:** Does accepted experience change useful effort allocation, or reinforce expensive
activity without useful return? Contrast reinforcement on/off in ordinary cells with actual
accepted activity, including metabolic and optical channels. Separate this from reflex-learning
on/off. Inspect response state, acquired bias, requested effort, accepted activity and incremental
work. Match present cues when attributing a difference to history rather than environment.

Use the existing matched-history, participant-removal, product-created-stimulus and withdrawal
fixtures as current-law mechanism references. Preserve the low-cue and outward-only failures.
In an ordinary neighborhood, compare responses to a naturally accumulating substrate or declining
route; a nonzero bias alone is not a positive outcome. Off retains state: on → off → on checks
state/control semantics, while independent rewound arms estimate effects.

Test `strategicGain` only as a separate clock/cost intervention. Use `learningRetention=0` for
birth transmission, not as a substitute for disabling lifetime response. Neither static learning
nor reflex-learning-off freezes learning while retaining reinforcement influence; that precise
diagnostic would need an explicit isolated-copy intervention. Decision: retain the mechanism,
identify a physical/sensory bottleneck, or investigate self-reinforcing waste in a named context.

### B. Strategic history, context, learning gain and copied memory

**Question:** What does slow history add beyond current reflex inputs, and which part pays?
Compare context-on/off, long-mean clamp, neutral gain, strategic-learning-off and whole-strategy-off
from S. Read context, long memories, surprise/volatility, gain, inputs, effort and separate learning
expenses. The clamp command targets current cells; verify inheritance and descendant settings
before treating it as a persistent population-wide ablation.

Use one finite interruption that requires stored history and one sustained change. Price each
cell's inherited long timescale against the actual interruption, cue and available alternative.
Context-on/off retains slow costs and answers influence; whole-strategy-off answers net operating
value. A frozen forward sensitivity is an action-path check, not survival or fitness evidence.

Birth-local acquired weights, copied strategic hidden/long state and newborn reflex resets are
different channels. `learningRetention=0` does not disable copied strategic memory. A matched
newborn-state reset and a private-byte/recurrence mask are isolated diagnostic cases without
dedicated config flags. Decision: distinguish useful history, inappropriate timing, redundant
information, inaccessible action and learning cost rather than prescribing a larger network.

Contact-read neighbor displays need their own diagnostic input mask with contact geometry,
chemistry and costs retained. There is no independent live social-display flag. Compare that
path separately from hearing before attributing a neighborhood response to either carrier.

### C. Finite chemical recognition and broad processing

**Question:** Is breadth coming from broad per-site recognition, several selective routes,
private cycling or exposure history, and which inherited distinctions change returns?
Reduce `chemicalRelationships`, funded body capacities, internal/external concentrations,
accepted transport and per-chemical gross histories. Compare contemporaneous neighboring cells
without equating a route label to a diet or an unfunded key to an expressed capability.

For a selected ordinary descendant with retained ancestor alleles, revert one funded key on an
isolated matched copy using the existing native single-locus fork method. Keep private state and
materials; declare its equal mutation/assimilation freeze in both arms. If ancestor payloads are
unavailable, do not invent them. A handcrafted key contrast answers an opportunity question only.
Measure changed uptake/protection, processing, injury, net work and neighbor consequences.

Use keyed/radial startup only for the representation's total effect. Check all seventeen consumers
against finite affinity bounds; do not reinstate universal recognition. Assess accessible chemical
changes and inherited expression through actual births, with no breadth tax or chosen role count.

### D. Chemical stigmergy, metabolic succession and public-food use

**Question:** Does one cell's chemical action create/remove a cue that changes another's paid
behavior, and who gains or loses? Select a producer/intermediate/receiver from accepted flows
and reachable exact products. Compare ordinary export, held producer export, and held receiver
response in an isolated physical neighborhood. Selective export and cue masking lack HTTP flags;
use declared diagnostic controllers, not a fictional global stigmergy switch.

Trace export → changed local material → receptor reading → altered effort → accepted uptake and
conversion → both cells' returns. Preserve shared donor competition and pay for exported material.
Test removal of the downstream participant for product accumulation and upstream consequences.
Do not claim unique molecular provenance from correlated flow profiles. A dependency, behavioral
feedback and mutual cost recovery are three separate outcomes.

Include the current-law public-conversion path: compare a controlled diagnostic with public
processing withheld and the same material boundary, or a structural `weatheringRate` contrast.
That is not an available live switch. Keep the earlier failed public-food hypothesis negative;
multiple nonseed conversions alone cannot overturn it. Decision: locate a causal dependency,
exploitative interaction, useful succession or missing producer return.

### E. Declining returns, persistence, movement, exchange and founding

**Question:** Do cells remain through short interruptions but redirect activity or leave when
returns persistently deteriorate and an affordable alternative exists? Compare reinforcement,
context and gain individually. Add their 2×2 interaction only if the separate results or actual
clock coupling warrant it. Use actual season/renewal changes first, and a declared source epoch
in a constructed world if the natural window lacks a discriminating reversal.

Price leaving and staying from local readable cues, reserves, food-access edges, terrain load,
travel, repair and destination opportunity. Use swapped origin/destination assignments for a
constructed spatial case. An alternative that is inaccessible or unaffordable cannot diagnose
a controller's failure to switch. A profitable homeostatic colony is a valid reference.

Record linked cell/descendant IDs, births/deaths, periodic paths and moving reservoir positions.
Match habitats spatially through time rather than relying on nearest-source labels or observer
colony IDs. Distinguish departure, arrival, persistence/reproduction in an established habitat,
and reproduction after arrival at a previously unoccupied habitat. Occupancy growth is not a
founding certificate. Existing coarse history cannot establish every link; use bounded native
study traces around a selected event or report exchange/founding unmeasured. Current v56 has
relaxation but no separately implemented spatial commitment/abandonment system to toggle.

### F. Death recycling and source-local distress

**Question:** Does local recovery improve survivor return/replacement when remote growth masks
local decline? Use three rewound arms: local recovery, global response, and recovery disabled.
Include a balanced local-turnover negative case. Select an overlap-eligible natural decline or
the existing isolated-decline/healthy-neighbor fixture; absent eligible deaths is an inactive
control, not evidence that recovery is useless.

Trace lost bound-body chemistry → overlap-normalized admission → stored contents → ordinary
release → actual survivor intake/processing → expenses, endurance and replacement. Observe free
spill in every arm. Do not add admitted stock to released stock or infer all intake came from
the corpse. Track finite contents separately from the external supply profile and actual imports.
Recovery-off retains prior recovered contents; start before admission for a clean event contrast.

Relate `mortalityMemory` and `mortalityHalfResponse` to measured decline and delivery timescales;
test one derived alternative only after the chain identifies a response limitation. Preserve full
startup batches and nominal refill policy. Decision: local masking, eligibility, composition,
delay, competition or a genuine survivor-return effect; more recovered material is not rescue.

### G. Sunlight, geographic shade, cover, photoreception and emission

**Question:** Which optical investments produce a physical or informational return for the
payer? Split sensing, geographic attenuation, builder activity and emission into separate arms.
Choose a chemical condition where exposure can change the value of a retained intermediate,
and a low-opportunity comparison. Count existing film and ongoing replacement; stopping building
does not remove the shelter already bought.

For photoreception, compare action/return with exposure and funded bodies matched. For cover,
follow deposit/recovery, opacity, actual public/private conversion, injury, material opportunity
cost, deposition expense and replacement. Only a startup no-builder contrast estimates removed
body investment. A short benefit that excludes washout/photochemical replacement stays a short
benefit, not sustained shelter profit.

For emission, report paid work, material-weighted allocation, captured emitted work and loss
