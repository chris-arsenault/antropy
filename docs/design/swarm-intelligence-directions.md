# Swarm intelligence design directions

**Status:** Conditional paths and operator controls delivered locally October 13, 2026.
The [execution plan](../plans/CONDITIONAL-ECOLOGY-PLAN.md#execution-expansions) records current-law
chemical succession, metabolic alternatives, paid history and message-return checks, with limits.
Evolved coordination, sustained cooperation and inter-colony founding remain research questions.

Develop conditional specialization and coordination through actions that change local
opportunities. Antropy already supplies chemical transformations, contested material access,
heritable capacities and paid controllers. The strongest starting point is chemical stigmergy:
one cell changes its surroundings, another responds, and their actions alter what becomes
worthwhile next.

These directions apply ideas from Bonabeau, Dorigo and Theraulaz's
[*Swarm Intelligence: From Natural to Artificial Systems*](https://academic.oup.com/book/40811)
(1999). The book's biological mechanisms and engineering algorithms have different purposes.
Antropy's cells reproduce independently; a benefit to a neighborhood does not automatically
repay the cell that produces it. Cooperation, competition, eavesdropping and withdrawal are all
possible consequences. Homeostasis remains a valid outcome.

## Common mathematical and physical basis

Use the existing local chemical mixture, inherited recognition and transformation profiles,
funded body capacities, neural state and action-effort vector. The controller selects bounded
efforts; recognition and donor allocation determine accepted transfers; exact chemical actions
determine products and work. Changed surroundings supply later receptor readings. This
composition already permits feedback between cells without a separate coordination layer.

Private experience acts through the existing bounded neural trace law and recurrent state.
When enabled, the slow strategic layer supplies held context and learning gain using the same
neural operator. It changes activity within inherited capacities. Body proportions and complete
chemical capabilities remain fixed genetically at birth; these directions do not restore
construction, refitting or living-cell gene transfer.

The [composed laws](chemistry/composed-runtime.md), [controller contract](controller.md) and
[funded bodies](funded-bodies.md) own those operations. Any later mathematical revision needs
a demonstrated missing relationship and one shared operation over the relevant state. Do not
introduce chemical-specific probabilities, task thresholds, a breadth tax, role rewards or a
global fitness signal to obtain a desired community.

## Chemical stigmergy and metabolic succession

**Book basis:** Chapters 1 and 3 describe coordination through environmental changes, including
actions that remove a stimulus and actions that create the conditions for a subsequent action.
Chapter 6 extends this to structures that constrain what can happen next.

**Proposed application:** Make chemical consequences the carrier of coordination. A conversion
and export by one cell can create a substrate, exposure or obstruction for another. The second
cell's uptake and conversion can then remove that condition or create a different one. A
sequence of activities could follow from changing mixtures without a shared clock, stage label
or task assignment. The same interaction can benefit one genotype and harm another.

The relevant state is actual local material, including its identity and amount. Its persistence
comes from existing transport, diffusion, transformations and loss. Information has no separate
free carrier: producing, exporting or intercepting material retains its ordinary accounts.
Terrain and reservoir geometry constrain encounters; they do not prescribe the sequence or
number of participating cells.

**Design decisions:** Identify an accessible sequence of exact transformations and a local
readable change that can alter an ordinary action. Calculate whether each participating cell
can recover its transport, conversion and maintenance costs. Distinguish incidental use of
another cell's output from an action that responds to that output. A mutually beneficial cycle
is one possible result; a producer subsidizing an exploiter is another.

**Discriminating comparison:** Begin with a constructed producer and consumer sharing a small
physical neighborhood, with fixed genotypes and declared learning settings. Follow actual
export, accepted uptake and net return. A matched intervention on the producer's export tests
the chemical dependency; holding the receiver's relevant response constant tests whether the
interaction also changes behavior. Preserve donor limits and count the producer's costs in
both interpretations. A dependency alone establishes cross-feeding, not adaptive coordination.

This direction develops the interaction questions in
[chemical keys and broad processing](chemical-key-specialization.md). Earlier
[constructed food-chain findings](../rugged-community-results.md) establish a possible dependency
under their recorded law and conditions; a new comparison must use the current finite binding
partition. Persistent complementary roles remain an open ecological question.

## Experience dependent allocation within inherited capacities

**Book basis:** Chapter 3 shows how response thresholds and experience can produce specialization
and replacement. Its activity-reinforcement model strengthens a task tendency through use;
that mechanism does not itself establish a beneficial return.

**Proposed application:** Let experience change the allocation of effort among existing enzyme
programs and transporters. A cell could favor one route while its substrate is available, then
redirect activity when the local mixture or energetic consequence changes. Specialization
would describe conditional behavior within a genotype's repertoire, alongside inherited
differences between genotypes.

Use the existing recurrent state, private plasticity and, where enabled, strategic context.
Realized actuator activity supplies execution feedback relative to capacity, but it is not a
measurement of marginal benefit. Energy changes also combine several simultaneous causes.
Blindly reinforcing the most active enzyme could therefore preserve wasteful cycling. The
design question is whether the current shared learning law and available history connect a
relevant chemical cue to a change in effort that improves funded return.

**Design decisions:** Choose a physical situation where the same inherited repertoire supports
two differently valuable allocations as conditions change. Establish what an immediate reflex
can infer and what requires experience. Separate recurrence, learned traces and slow context
before selecting any revision; adding per-enzyme threshold dynamics would duplicate control
without resolving the benefit question.

**Discriminating comparison:** Change supply once in a bounded single-cell fixture with two
usable routes. Compare a responsive controller with a diagnostic intervention that holds the
candidate adaptive state fixed while retaining the same physical capacities and accounting
for control costs. Measure cue, effort, accepted conversion and net return before and after
the change. A constructed successful response establishes an available opportunity; inheritance
and evolutionary discovery are separate questions.

The [controller](controller.md), [strategic controller](strategic-controller.md) and
[strategy usefulness direction](vocalization-and-strategy-usefulness.md) retain ownership of
neural representation and any selected learning revision.

## Recovery from declining returns

**Book basis:** Chapter 1 includes negative feedback among the ingredients of self-organization.
Chapter 2's bridge experiments show that reinforcement can retain an established route after
a shorter alternative becomes available.

**Proposed application:** Couple persistence to changing local returns. Depletion, crowding,
product accumulation or increased exposure can undermine an established activity. Those
changes could reduce commitment to a chemical route or make movement worthwhile. Useful
persistence should survive a brief interruption without preventing a response to sustained
deterioration.

The relevant operations already include finite supply, shared donor competition, chemical
turnover and paid movement. Receptor changes and energy history can expose declining returns;
motor load reports geographic resistance, rather than a destination or direct measure of
progress. The slow controller's history may help distinguish a transient shortage from a
persistent one. Compare environmental turnover and paid memory timescales before proposing
another forgetting rate or an escape probability.

**Design decisions:** Identify a decline that the cell can actually sense and an affordable
alternative that its existing repertoire can exploit. Determine whether an apparent failure
to switch reflects missing information, inaccessible material, movement cost or reinforced
behavior. Nearby reservoirs are expected habitats; distance from them is not a benefit by
itself. Remaining in a productive colony can be the appropriate response.

**Discriminating comparison:** Present one initially useful local opportunity and an accessible
alternative, then reverse their relative returns through a declared physical supply change.
Compare return before and after the reversal, including travel and control expense. Inspect
cue and effort changes before interpreting a failure as a memory defect. Use swapped starting
assignments if location could explain the result. Do not extend the run merely to obtain a
departure.

This direction connects [local resource seasons](local-resource-seasons.md), the
[strategic controller](strategic-controller.md) and the
[ecology review guide](../ecology-review-guide.md). Temporal exchange and founding matter for
later ecological assessment; a near/far snapshot cannot establish either.

## Communication beyond local chemical cues

**Book basis:** Chapters 1 and 6 show how environmental changes mediate interactions. They
motivate asking which additional information requires a separate message; they do not
establish a benefit for Antropy's byte-based vocalization.

**Proposed application:** Reserve a possible role for speech where cells have different local
information. A speaker might detect a change before a listener can encounter its chemical
consequences. A received impulse could alter the listener's transport effort, movement or
held context soon enough to improve its return. The content and response must remain
evolvable; no byte is assigned a food, danger or recruitment meaning.

Use the existing funded mouth and ear capacities, directional impulses and ordinary neural
inputs. Compare message arrival with chemical transport and environmental change: a redundant
or late message has little value. Account separately for the listener's gain and the speaker's
speech cost. A return to the speaker or its descendants might follow through altered local
material conditions, but it must be identified rather than supplied as a cooperation reward.
Silence and eavesdropping remain viable responses.

**Design decisions:** Specify the speaker's information advantage, the listener's actionable
choice and the physical route by which speaking could repay its cost. Determine whether the
current reception representation preserves the relevant distinction under interference. A
larger network or a richer alphabet is justified only by a missing usable distinction.

**Discriminating comparison:** Use a small neighborhood with a declared local cue available to
the speaker earlier than to the listener. Compare ordinary delivery with a matched intervention
that removes the message's information while retaining comparable exposure and signaling
expense. Trace the received input through the changed action to recipient return, then examine
the speaker's resulting material and work balance. Listener benefit alone does not establish
a sustainable incentive to speak.

The [vocalization and strategy usefulness direction](vocalization-and-strategy-usefulness.md)
owns any selected communication revision. Nonzero utterance counts or varying context do not
establish the proposed information advantage.

## Recommended starting point

Start with chemical stigmergy coupled to conditional allocation. It uses ordinary chemistry
and reflex control and can be studied without enabling optional speech or strategic machinery.
First establish a funded chemical dependency, then whether available local feedback changes
activity usefully. Introduce the slow layer only for a decision whose timing needs history;
consider speech when an actual information gap remains.

Before any run, calculate resource budgets with the existing
[resource economy](chemistry/resource-economy.md), read relevant negative findings, and declare
initial conditions, competing explanations, causal prediction, decision, learning settings and
run limits. Reuse the existing native engine and diagnostic runners. Keep generated captures
local under the [experiment policy](../../AGENTS.md#experiment-operating-policy). These short
comparisons choose between design explanations; they do not require a population campaign or
certify long-term ecological success. Any selected implementation remains a later scoped work order.
