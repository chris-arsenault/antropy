# Rugged interaction: exported intermediate, fuel and harm

**Status:** Local v52 constructed physical result, October3,2026. The same exported chemical
funds a changed neighbor and imposes an unfunded burden on its parent genotype. This establishes
a causal opportunity in the shared simulation. It does not establish spontaneous evolution or
persistent coexistence. No production physics, costs, seed or lambda were tuned to this result.
The live server remains v50. No publication or deployment was performed.

## Result

The producer processes0→136 and exports136. A neighbor with an enzyme key recognizing136
processes136→128, pays transport, maintenance, repair and assembly, then divides. The neighbor's
parent key recognizes128 and processes136 poorly. In the same shared world that parent dies,
while the changed neighbor grows and divides. Swapping their positions preserves this result.

A separate comparison holds food constant and removes the producer and competition. Adding136
at a concentration observed in the shared world kills the unprotected parent by starvation.
Changing only its membrane key reduces exposure and repair costs and delays that outcome beyond
the declared horizon, while leaving its funded work negative. The same intermediate therefore
has opposite net consequences depending on inherited recognition. There is no toxin, food,
cooperation or role label in the runtime.

`W = living usable work + cumulative paid assembly work − initial usable work` measures retained
work and funded assembly after ongoing expenses. It does not count material potential, absorbed
external work, population or gross reaction volume as a benefit. It excludes dissipated division
work and overflow; it is a conservative allocation measure, not lifetime fitness. Terminal
comparisons end at the registered horizon or extinction; early common-time observations are
retained separately. Fission ends a parent record and creates two daughters; births are not
generations or ecological deaths.

## Mutation distribution and implementation changes

The new read-only single-locus probe uses the existing reflected heavy-tail operator at a
uniformly selected one of17×9 expressed-key scalar loci, conditional on a mutation event. It
does not mutate a whole child or install a genotype. In128 fixed-seed events per ordinary
founder type, the maximum affinity change over chemical space has median0.0111 and90th

give the same magnitudes under relabeling and the same random draws; these are not512
independent replications or probabilities of adaptive gain. Sparse profiles use the production
cutoff. This measures a broad distribution containing many small effects and some large ones.

The existing binding budget now also reports `heldLightYield`, using the same observed optical
allocation and retained-composition signal as production physiology. Historical unit-light
ceilings remain explicitly labeled. At zero free inventory the processing calculation starts
with no substrate, so its initial processing surplus is negative even for a subsequently
profitable route. Neither analytical ceiling predicts the changing live trajectory.

No governing-law repair was needed: the previous failed transformation was a poor funded
opportunity, not evidence that affinity changes could never create one. The kernel's shared
binding, work accounts and ordinary consumers already produce the relationship when tested
with a budgeted action chain. These diagnostic additions leave checkpoint formatv52 unchanged.
