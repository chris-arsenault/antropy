# Source-to-design coverage

The archive preserves supplied text verbatim. This map records where its ideas live after the 2D
replacement; it does not claim that retired implementations survived.

<a id="coverage-method"></a>

## Coverage method

Every supplied source subsection is navigable from the [source index](../sources/README.md). The
tables below assign each source family to one or more current design owners and one disposition:
current principle, delivered 2D mechanism, backlog question, or OBE implementation.

## Appendix D and digging seed

The excavation ladder, action parity, body resolution, carriers, construction costs,
morphometrics, and function coupling belong to [environment](environment.md),
[controller](controller.md), and [experimentation](experimentation.md). Digging and the exact
construction/cargo seed are OBE as code and backlog as evolutionary questions. Shape vocabulary
remains descriptive only.

<a id="coverage-e"></a>

## Appendix E and E2

The authored-nest food loop, staged colony biology, field navigation, separation of pathfinding
instruments from creature controllers, and transferability tiers belong to
[colony biology](colony-biology.md), [controller](controller.md), and
[experimentation](experimentation.md). The 2D single-forager is the new foundation; no old ladder
certification transfers automatically.

<a id="coverage-f"></a>

## Appendix F

Broad viable space, affordance audits, external memory, additional carrier candidates, simple
programmed policy, temporal input, attention, and audit discipline belong to
[controller](controller.md), [environment](environment.md), and
[experimentation](experimentation.md). The current stateless policy resolves the baseline without
recurrence; richer mechanisms remain conditional on measured gaps.

<a id="coverage-g"></a>

## Appendix G

Resource floors, metabolic depression, edible brood, queen reserves, sire portfolios,
activity-scaled threats, resilience curves, and failure cautions belong to
[colony biology](colony-biology.md), [environment](environment.md),
[experimentation](experimentation.md), and [advanced systems](advanced.md). Measure the untreated
2D colony before admitting any floor.

<a id="coverage-h"></a>

## Appendix H

Scentless search, escalation by measured failure, directional exit cues, memory-based search, and
the record of prior over-design belong to [controller](controller.md) and
[experimentation](experimentation.md). Its former spatial policy is invalidated; its rule to fill
an existing branch before adding mechanisms remains current.
