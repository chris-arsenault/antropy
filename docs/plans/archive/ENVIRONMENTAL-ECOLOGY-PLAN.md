### September 19 correction: compose the axes

The user rejected v29's rapid, nearly one-axis sweep. Its displayed mean canceled the
mixed harmonic exactly: `(l0+l1)/2 = 1+c*(cos(u-f)+cos(v-s))/4`. The remaining X phase
ran twelve times faster than Y. Several independent periods did not supply the requested
composition, and the short opportunity probes did not validate its visible evolution.

V30 replaces that additive candidate with tensor products on the same periodic XY domain:

```
a = u - phaseFast
b = v - phaseSlow
m = phaseModulation
l0 = 1 + c*cos(a)*cos(b-m)
l1 = 1 + c*cos(b)*cos(a-m)
```

The slow-axis factor changes the amplitude and sign of the sweep, rather than adding a
brightness offset. The two response components retain different local returns, and their
displayed mean retains all three phases. At m=0 the mean is `1+c*cos(a)*cos(b)`;
a quadrature of b suppresses the X oscillation while a half-turn reverses it. This is
a product of functions on two circles, evaluated through the existing sine/cosine vectors.
Translations carry the coordinate phases, and simultaneous reflection carries all three
angles. There is no new space coordinate, per-chemical rule, random forcing or control.

Each product lies in[-1,1] and has spatial mean zero, so response bounds[1-c,1+c], mean1,
the work ceiling and all ordinary work consumers remain unchanged. The separable axis
caches remain O(nx+ny), with O(1) arithmetic and no per-node trigonometry. Products add
sum/difference frequencies; this is still periodic external forcing, not chaos.

### Selected mathematical shape

Use a bounded low-rank periodic forcing field. Three independent circular phases supply
a primary sweep, slower cross-direction variation and slow modulation of their relative
phase. Two non-collinear integer wavevectors describe directions on the existing torus.
This is an artificial illumination model, not a radiation or atmosphere solver.

Let `r=(x/width,y/height)`, `u=2*pi*k0 dot r`, `v=2*pi*k1 dot r`, and let the three
phases advance with their own periods in model seconds. A concrete candidate basis is:

```
b0 = cos(u - phaseFast)
b1 = cos(v - phaseSlow)
b2 = cos(u + v - phaseFast - phaseSlow - phaseModulation)

l0 = 1 + contrast * (b0 + b2) / 2
l1 = 1 + contrast * (b1 - b2) / 2
D  = diag(l0, l1)
```

`0 <= contrast <= 1` implies `0 <= l_k <= 2`. Each response has geographic mean one
on a resolved full torus; the cross term changes their relative strength. This uses
three spatial harmonics rather than a high-resolution weather simulation. The simplest
initial wavevectors are `(1,0)` and `(0,1)`; they select forcing directions, not chemical
coordinates or population locations. The mixed harmonic is their sum. No cluster centers,
source identities or cell positions determine the phases or wavelengths.

The two channels act on the **existing material-response basis**. They are not a new
pair of chemical species, named wavelengths or a mapping of geographic x/y onto chemical
x/y. Their purpose is to vary relative transformation returns. A universal multiplier
would vary energy quantity while leaving many powered pathways ranked in the same order.
Two independently varying components can change that ranking using the already compiled
coefficients. The differential mixed harmonic supplies one shared coupling between them;
there are no per-species illumination rates or preferred waste chemicals.

Supply the local drive `B_light = D B`, and change external work to:

```
w_e = epsilon * max(0, a_e dot B_light)
available_e = potentialDrop_e + w_e
```

Keep cellular efficiency, catalytic cost, occupancy, recognition and frozen-work funding.
Abiotic branch engagement and exposure still use the original B and current impedance
law; only branch affordability and its external-work/heat account use `B_light`.
Illumination is an energy input, not an extra kinetic multiplier, force or transport pass.
Changing work may indirectly admit an uphill abiotic branch or a paid cellular return.
Sources sample the same field over their existing footprint and retain their release,
renewal and processing boundaries. Cells receive the same coupling, never free work added
directly to their usable-energy stores.

Local nonlinearity comes from actual material: secretion, uptake, transformation and bodies
change H; normalized H changes which reactions couple to the supplied drive; those reactions
then change material again. Existing impedance changes abiotic exposure and transport.
Do not add another attenuation factor to cellular yield merely to call it shade, or count
the existing impedance attenuation twice. This proposal does not yet model geometric shadow.

The selected rational periods have a common cycle of1,116,000model seconds; this is
multi-periodic forcing, not by itself deterministic chaos. Coupled ecological feedback can produce irregular local outcomes,
but that is not established by summing harmonics. The requested several interacting axes
are supplied explicitly. Do not add white noise or a separate chaotic attractor to manufacture
unpredictability. If the coupled feature merely produces synchronous population oscillation,
it has missed the intended differential opportunity even if the light display is attractive.

## Recommended feature

Build one coupled environmental feedback, with three interacting parts:

1. **Local environmental variation.** A smooth, slowly varying abiotic condition creates
   different exposure or chemical-persistence conditions across the periodic XY world.
   Its timescale must allow both residence and relocation to matter. It carries no lineage
   information and makes no response to population success.
2. **Chemistry outside cells.** Local conditions change how extracellular material persists
   or transforms. Prefer bounded generic chemical mappings and existing shared profiles.
   A candidate is environment-dependent conversion that preserves material and dissipates
   accounted potential. No named food/toxin roles, free work or all-pairs reaction table.
3. **Paid habitat modification.** Exported material changes the local response to those
   conditions, then disperses, weathers or can be consumed and transformed. A candidate
   is a deposit that protects useful chemistry from exposure while impeding access or
   movement. Its owner has no exclusive entitlement; neighbors can benefit too.

The causal chain is: paid production and export → persistent local chemistry → changed
delivery or exposure → changed net return on an action or body investment. The proposal
includes circumstances where investment loses its advantage, such as mild exposure,
departure, degradation or access costs. Small deposits need a possible incremental return;
a fully formed shelter being useful is insufficient.

The recommended first implementation uses chemical deposits as the modifiable medium,
without a separate solid terrain grid. Microclimate here describes local environmental
conditions, not a temperature simulation. M0 selects the actual rates, maps and couplings
from their ecological effects and cost. It may revise the proposed weathering mechanism if
the budget does not work, while retaining all three relationships above.

Increasing global impedance alone is inadequate: it does not supply the missing feedback
and could erase mobility. A full binding reservoir, extracellular enzyme system or new body
stock is justified only if the selected interaction needs it; do not install all of them
as speculative infrastructure. Conversely, existing coefficients are not a reason to patch
around a representation that cannot express the selected interaction.
