## Settled decisions and calibration

- Preserve current 52 reflex channels. Hearing adds 27 impulse channels, context adds four:
  total 83. Strategy uses nine history channels plus activity/diversity (33 transformed values),
  11 self/history, 11 local rhythms/lifecycles and six contact/noise inputs: total 61.
- The private byte remains reflex-owned and strategic read-only. Speech uses its own eight
  output signs and one effort; it never automatically broadcasts or overwrites the byte.
- Mouth/ear follow existing genetic body proportions and shared mutation. Mouth uses the
  ordinary actuator work rate; ear uses receptor gain and damage convention. Choose and record
  one dimensional work/area coefficient against short resource budgets, not signaling payoff.
- Integrate hearing as an exactly-once event measure, not a held or time-averaged cue.
  Freeze positions, heading and ear gain at batch delivery. Use periodic local membership,
  no listener quotas, no recipient energy and no packet history.
- Reuse the bounded neural and trace operators for strategy: eight hidden units, five outputs,
  eight physiology intervals, one transformed long timescale and per-unit retention. Context
  is held; gain in [0,2] scales reflex learning rate and its paid work together.
- Copy strategic hidden state, long memories and context at birth. Reset reflex hidden state,
  private byte and pending hearing for newborns; budding parents retain private experience.
- Advance the physical format once for the complete feature, rejecting older formats. No
  running server update or reset follows implicitly from this local implementation.

Technical calibration used dimensional resource bounds, protected work reservations and bounded
mechanics checks. The [delivery evidence](../utterances-and-strategy-results.md) records the
coefficient, reach, strategic interval, founder relays and measured operating limits.
