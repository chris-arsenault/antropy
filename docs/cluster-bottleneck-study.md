## Selected continuation

The first measurements make adhesion a plausible but modest contributor: removing it increases
the sum of one-step travel by 16.5% in the saved world and 11.5% in the constructed clump, at
identical motor payment. It does not reveal a population-wide mechanical lock. The constructed
clump loses most reaction income without neighboring bodies, whereas the mature saved world
retains about 84% of its net reaction income. The earlier small fixture exaggerated dependence
on neighbors relative to the mature world.

Run the two original close-placement cases with adhesion zero and all other starting conditions
unchanged. Use `clusterPlacement.ts ROOT unbound`; keep original control results. Two cases,
1,500 ticks/120 seconds each, same frozen learning and mutation, no new genotypes or food.
These check whether a modest immediate change accumulates into useful dispersal while preserving
feeding and growth. Compare spatial extent, pairwise spacing, contact, travel per organism time,
survival and intake. Food and initial stocks must match the existing control files.
If the benefit is negligible, do not tune an adhesion sweep. If it materially changes dispersal,
use the result to recommend a bounded change in mechanical coupling rather than more shade.

## Historical finding and withdrawn direction

**Withdrawn recommendation: prioritize accumulated numerical participation for public chemistry.** Slow physical reactions
should execute less often with accounted elapsed exposure, rather than disappear because every
individual update is below the performance cutoff. This is the next recommended implementation,
not a production change delivered by this investigation. It uses the existing chemical law and
persistent regional ownership; it adds no food identity, density reward or finite sunlight rule.

In the saved live world, one frozen 0.8-second public interval converted 0.06385 material with
the production operator, versus 0.10834 with the existing scalar operator without the skip
floor: 41.1% of permitted conversion was omitted. Of 20,071 occupied nodes with positive
reference conversion, 16,796 (83.7%) performed none. There were 52,601 occupied nodes in total;
the remaining 32,530 lacked positive reference conversion and are not counted as skipped.
Maximum reference energy/material residual was 1.31×10⁻¹⁴. The inspector's unchanged-checkpoint
assertion passed. These are solar-only frozen rates, not cumulative live flows or predicted
food reaching cells.

The omission occurs both within and outside body footprints: 56.4% and 37.5% of reference
conversion respectively. Thus this is not evidence that all suppression occurs away from
clumps. Eligibility depends on each chemical's concentration and the local medium, not just
total biomass. In the transplanted habitat, all 256 nodes were skipped; their combined reference
conversion was 0.001816 per interval. That absolute amount is small and does not explain the
whole-cell deaths by itself. It identifies the loss of slow chemical pathways.

### Why this changes behavior

The current gate is in `Climate::convert_funded` and `weathering::minimum_donor`. Let F be
the material resolution (concentration floor times mesh area), q the donor material, and
h the rate times elapsed interval times shielding times signal strength times incident light.
The donor pair is skipped when both amounts are at most `F/min(2h,1)`. This is a bounded
per-update work elimination rule. The next physiological interval starts the same calculation
again; the skipped reaction progress is not accumulated. Persistent weak reactions can therefore
remain absent across many intervals. Separately, generated products below F are pruned during
commit. Lowering only the donor gate would not resolve loss of small products.

The concrete implementation direction is to retain the shared resolution and sparse regions
but use a bounded accumulated error/exposure criterion for deferred public conversions and
their products. Reuse local clocks and invalidate them when donors, medium or light change.
Actual commits must still share frozen donors and close material/work accounts. Deferred work
is not stored free energy and cannot consume material that has already left. Preserve tiny
products until they either accumulate into active support or are handled by the explicit
bounded-loss policy; do not create a second unaccounted chemical inventory.

Check the implementation first against these same frozen inputs and a short dilute-mixture
continuation, then measure cost on the saved world. The decision criterion is materially
recovering the omitted slow activity within the operating budget, not a prescribed cluster
count or an evolved coexistence result. This is a numerical participation/scheduling change,
not a request to globally reduce the spatial mesh or blindly lower every cutoff.

### Other comparisons and corrections

- Removing adhesion increased immediate total travel by 16.5% in the saved world. Over the two
  reconstructed 1,500-tick comparisons it left 45/44 cells, versus 50/47 controls, with no
  cell more than six units from a neighbor. Mean pairwise separation increased only 9.1%/5.4%.
  This does not support weakening adhesion as the next general dispersal change.
- Actual-state probes removed the storage-overfill confound. Five of six imported; the
  large-clump cell in its own sampled dissolved mixture imported zero. One isolated cell
  reached the 300-tick horizon but had not established a continuing surplus.
- All four actual-state populations became extinct: original feedstock at ticks 189/141
  (close/spread), sampled mobile habitat at 699/269. These are finite, unrenewed, transplanted
  environments. They do not establish impossibility of solitary life or justify selecting
  a preferred winner. No horizon was extended.
- In the saved world, removing neighbor bodies from the frozen work signal retained about
  84% of population net reaction income. The small reconstructed clump retained only 14%.
  The previous experiment was not representative enough to recommend strengthening the
  dependence of cells on neighbors or weakening it across the whole runtime.

The governing goal remains different profitable inherited choices. Restoring slow public
chemical pathways is a plausible way to broaden the chemistry available to those choices.
Whether it produces more diffuse cells remains an experimental direction, not a delivery gate.
