# Light, shade and emission usefulness

**Status:** Open research direction — design incomplete, October 2, 2026.

The actual design remains to be completed. This document records the problem, intended
opportunity and unresolved decisions; it does not select new equations or an implementation.
The existing [light ecology](light-ecology.md), [photoreception](../photoreception.md) and
[composed runtime](chemistry/composed-runtime.md) remain the installed contracts.

## Purpose

Make illumination, shade and cell-paid emission consequential choices whose usefulness depends
on local chemistry and conditions. Possible returns include preserving useful intermediates,
avoiding harmful public transformations, timing activity and providing useful local optical
information or transformation work. The design should explain how a return reaches the paying
cell or its descendants and can plausibly repay sensing, movement, cover or emission costs.
It does not prescribe light-seeking, builders, lamp organisms or a particular community.

## Why this direction is open

The [v42 checks](../light-ecology-results.md) established funded sensory responses, cover
attenuation and detectable emission, but found no positive whole-cell shelter or lamp return.
The October 2 overnight v50 review again found that almost all paid emission became heat or
uncaptured work, with negligible emitted-work capture by chemistry. Retained cover was small
despite ongoing deposition. Final-checkpoint probes showed responses to light level, while
directional light reversal produced weak changes under those sampled conditions.

These observations motivate reconsidering usefulness. Emitted-work capture measures an energy
transfer, not the full value of an optical signal; low capture alone cannot dismiss information
benefits. Small standing cover does not establish that shade never helps. The overnight review
did not retain enough earlier controller and chemistry detail to reconstruct those benefits
throughout the night. Its local provenance is
`frontend/harness/artifacts/mortality-overnight-20261002/`, ticks 87,379–476,061;
the detailed surviving inspection is at tick 476,061.

## Design still to complete

- Identify when more light helps and when shade helps through the existing chemical algebra,
  public processing and private metabolism. Trace the effect to intake, reserves, growth or
  reduced injury rather than assuming darkness or brightness has intrinsic value.
- Separate static geographic shade, paid cell-made cover, light sensing and paid emission.
  Decide which physical or informational opportunity is missing in each, and how they compose.
- Explain the poor realized emission return: useful recipients and substrate, local reach,
  work allocation, timing and expressed controller effort are competing explanations. Select
  a coherent correction only after identifying the missing link.
- Define the cover investment and replacement budget, exposure change and conditional return
  using ordinary material owners and costs. Accepted low-volume pruning is not a new defect
  or an authorization to reopen numerical thresholds.
- Specify the chosen shared operation, units, bounds, ordinary configuration, persistence and
  bounded observations. Any new independent control needs a reason existing parameters cannot
  express the opportunity.

## Boundaries and completion

Keep the overhead, non-depleting sun, one scalar optical state, the periodic XY plane and
conservative material/work accounting. Emission pays from the emitter's usable work; a signal
does not grant a receiver free energy. Reuse funded bodies, ordinary controllers, regional
owners and the existing observation/harness paths. Do not assign roles, signal meanings,
preferred chemicals or ecological rewards.

Complete the design by choosing the physical and informational relationships and explaining
their costs and plausible conditional returns. Later implementation needs bounded checks of
those relationships and runtime integrity, not proof of evolved specialists or indefinite
ecological success. No new experiment campaign is selected by this direction.
