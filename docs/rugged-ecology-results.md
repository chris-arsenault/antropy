# Rugged recognition: inherited function and neighborhood consequences

**Status:** Local v52 ecology iteration and startup calibration, October3,2026.
The shared default is6 for new ordinary worlds. Existing saved configuration and the
live server world are unchanged. No publication or deployment occurred.

An ordinary inherited recognition change enabled a newborn to reproduce where restoring
one weight made it starve before division. A different naturally born change altered a
neighbor's chemical readings and movement; that neighbor starved beside the changed
recognizer and survived when one bias was restored. These results answer questions about
ways of living and effects on other organisms, beyond binding coefficients or processing
totals. They do not establish permanent ecological roles or superiority over smooth chemistry.

## Judgment and implementation

The [root plan](plans/RUGGED-ECOLOGY-PLAN.md) carries the outcome, four ecological questions,
decision rules and registrations. It supersedes the earlier habit of treating a short
assay or completed tests as completion of the requested iteration. Its live Sulion
guidance requires parent/descendant consequences, shared surroundings, neighbor effects
and actual renewal/replacement coverage at consequential decisions and resumes.

The implementation adds a native ordinary-world runner that reuses the sole physical
World and existing step-level study recorder. Births, deaths, genotypes and actual transfers
are streamed, including cells that die between samples. Local mixtures are sampled at
funded body footprints. Source identities/positions and stock changes accompany each
sample. Checkpoints retain private reflex and strategic state.

Counterfactual forks change one recognition scalar in a copied actual world, preserve
every cell's private experience and physical packet, and compile the same production
operators. Mutation and birth assimilation freeze equally in those comparisons; private
learning remains enabled. This corrects the previous method of extracting a descendant,
resetting its experience and replacing its environment. It is an explicit research
intervention, not production refitting or a second physical economy.

## A birth-local change opens reproduction

Cell551 / genotype507 is born at tick12,000, generation18, from cell525 / genotype481.
Its enzyme1 weight at locus6 changes from−0.9702400571924419 to+0.6266591286874159.
The parent's measured processing favored136 and104. The descendant processes substantial
134 and102 and divides in the unmodified ordinary record. Other genes also change at
birth, so the observed parent/child contrast alone cannot attribute this function to the weight.

The retained checkpoint is exactly at this birth. Two copies run1,600 additional ticks:
the actual genotype and that same genotype with only the weight restored. Starting
biomass, bound mixture, free inventory, private experience, every other gene, location
and surrounding organisms agree.

With the inherited weight, the focal cell divides at12,708. Restoring the weight changes
processing back toward136/104; the cell grows but starves at12,646 without reproducing.
The unchanged copy funds more than twice the clade's assembly over the full comparison,
including its offspring, but gross work is not the verdict: reproduction versus death
before division is the consequential difference.

Both new descendants subsequently starve, at13,001 and13,227. The ordinary mutable
world's selected branch also eventually ends. This is a temporary new reproductive
opportunity, not demonstrated invasion, adaptation of a persistent population or a
sustaining new lineage. New opportunities can disappear; this finding must retain that limit.

## Another inherited change changes a neighbor's fate

Cell63 / genotype19, born at2,612 from cell50 / genotype6, broadens enzyme2 through a
bias change−7 to−4.088893508585411. Its processing shifts from mainly0 toward substantial
0 and8 beside ordinary cells releasing8. At tick4,000 the full world is copied twice;
one copy restores only that bias. Both run2,000 additional ticks under the same diagnostic
inheritance policy, with all private state and neighbors preserved.

Both focal clades divide and retain two living descendants at tick6,000. The new bias
does not improve their reproduction in this comparison. It changes their chemical
processing and release into shared surroundings. Neighbor11 begins with the same genes,
experience, inventory and location in both copies. By4,200, before the focal division,
its chemical readings, steering and location have diverged. In the changed-key world
it takes a path into a depleted neighborhood and starves at4,869. In the restored-key
world it remains alive at6,000 with usable energy near its capacity.

This is a causal effect of changing another cell's recognition in an otherwise matched
starting world. The recorded sequence supports altered chemical cues followed by altered
movement and resource access. Later differences also include division timing, contacts,
source motion and endogenous feedback. It is not an isolated molecular toxicity assay,
proof of deliberate signaling, or proof that every imported molecule came from the focal cell.

The actual mutable descendants of cell63 undergo six divisions and six ecological deaths
over the longer record. One generation5 descendant remains at17,248 and still processes
substantial0 and8. That is continuing inheritance and replacement of an observed chemical
way of living, not proof of permanent coexistence or a fixed role for every descendant.

## Startup tuning decision

One registered stronger setting6 runs the same ordinary world to16,000 ticks, reaching
generation29 with all initial founders replaced and346 sampled refill jumps. Around
tick10,600–11,000, descendants sharing a source neighborhood and almost the same local
mixture express distinct routes: cell249 mainly processes136→135→119, while nearby
cell215 and subsequent descendants process0→114/113 or0→98/114. Actual local readings,
transfers, growth and divisions accompany those routes. Their presence is not inferred
from possible enzyme transformations or seeded identities.

Select6 for the direction's sharper between-cell recognition: a full-weight bit mismatch
crosses twice the logit distance of setting3, while the ordinary comparison retains
growth, replacement and differentiated activity after renewals. This is a mechanistic
operating choice supported by bounded ordinary behavior, not a measured optimum or a
claim that larger populations make an ecosystem more interesting. The baseline also
differentiates; these runs do not establish higher long-term diversity with6.

The one shared default changes in native Config and therefore ordinary browser/server
creation. No separate receptor, transporter, enzyme or membrane control was added.
Previously saved setting3 remains3. Explicit diagnostic settings and archived experiments
retain their declared values. Exact chemical actions, potential, accounts, mutation law,
source settings, founder roles and spatial geography do not change.
