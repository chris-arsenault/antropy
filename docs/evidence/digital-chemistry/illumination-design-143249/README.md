## What illumination should address

The cumulative external work for cellular reactions is 2,874,739.2; dissolved and source
conversion receive 1,637.4 and 1,023.6. Cellular reactions therefore account for 99.91%
of these three external-work entries. Supplied material's reference energy is a separate
account and is not included in that denominator. Overflow dissipates 869,516.5 work units
over the run. Both lineages' current median stores are near full.

Illumination limited to extracellular weathering would miss most existing energy collection.
A weak common brightness multiplier may leave private returns saturated. The selected plan
therefore modifies the shared cellular/abiotic external-work input and allows its two existing
material-response components to vary differently. Whether this changes the ordering of paid
returns must be established during implementation, without blacklisting the observed chemistry
or trying to eliminate the larger lineage.

The majority lineage's small, frequently motorless bodies and the minority's larger powered
bodies provide concrete contrasting candidates for short future opportunity checks. Preserve
their differentiation; neither an optimal generalist nor global convergence is established
by this endpoint. Energy saturation is a design consideration, not authorization to impose
a new world power budget or rewrite funded bodies.
