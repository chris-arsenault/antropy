## Outcome and decisions

Deliver a specific activity-dependent allocation mechanism, rather than describe existing
reflexes as research implementation. Bonabeau, Dorigo and Theraulaz, printed pp. 113 and
129–134, connect task performance to changed local demand and changing responsiveness.
Use-dependent responsiveness and inactivity relaxation must occur in ordinary mutable cells.
Physical chemistry supplies demand and its removal/creation; no task labels or group reward
enter the controller. Genotypes and body proportions remain immutable during life.

The selected extension is controller-owned plastic output bias for the fourteen actuators
already reporting accepted signed activity. One bounded affine update uses the existing
heritable reflex learning rate, paid learning time and plasticity magnitude. Acquired bias
participates in the ordinary incoming neural-row budget. Accepted activity reinforces its
direction; inactivity relaxes it. Requested but unfunded activity does not reinforce itself.
The same operation covers transport, enzyme programs, building and emission. This is a
performance-reinforcement mechanism, not an estimator of marginal benefit.

Private state persists in physical checkpoints and resets at birth after permitted birth-local
assimilation into inherited output bias. An independent live switch removes its influence and
incremental expense while retaining state. The existing learning switch freezes all private
plasticity. Price added learned biases by their count relative to the existing recurrent
weights and inference parameters, using existing work prices rather than a new free process.
Physical format changes without migration. The authorized publication replaces the v55 world
with a fresh v56 world through the normal pipeline.
