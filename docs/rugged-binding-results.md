# Rugged binding candidate: October 2 results

Scope correction: this square tests importer-dependent growth epistasis, not new fuel,
protection loss or distinct chemical roles. The later [chemical-consequence assay](chemical-consequence-results.md)
directly tests those physiological consequences: membrane protection passes, but funded new
fuel use fails. Neither result demonstrates co-located chemical roles.

The constructed two-locus square produces reciprocal sign epistasis in net funded biomass
return at lambda3. Both endpoints outperform both single mutants after300 ticks, and the
ordering survives1000 ticks of shared access in both registered placements. The lambda0.25
control fails the square becauseG11 is worse than both intermediates. This supports a local
complementarity mechanism; it does not select the complete production research direction or
establish evolved roles, mutual invasion, or sustained coexistence.

The [execution plan](plans/RUGGED-BINDING-CANDIDATE-PLAN.md) fixes the scope, derivation,
contrasts, horizons and accounts. The [direction](design/rugged-interaction.md) retains its
broader requirements. Fourteen registered runs used exactly5600 world ticks, no tuning or

their declared horizon without extinction or execution failure.

## Implemented candidate

Physical v51 stores optional inherited keys in immutable chemical machinery. All four consumers
(receptors, transporters, enzymes and membrane) use the same compiled sigmoid of an additive
eight-spin score. Signed weights have domain[-1,1], bias[-9,9] and world-wide positive
`bindingLambda` at most64. Relative support below1e-4 of the maximum affinity is omitted.
Unchanged compiled rows remain shared; changing lambda invalidates them. Mutation uses the
existing shared heavy-tailed operator, with bias expressed in its nine-unit domain. Program
duplication copies its recognition key as well as its exact transformation action.

The fixture enables keys through ordinary genotype installation and uses the existing physical
World, paid diagnostic RNNs, transport, reactions, growth and conservative reproduction. There
is no chemical reward or observer score in those laws. Ordinary founders retain their radial
recognition. The candidate is active in its harness fixture; the live server remainsv50.
