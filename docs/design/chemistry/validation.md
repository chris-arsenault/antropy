## Initial nutrition probe

Question: can installed import/enzyme machinery pay maintenance and construction from finite
external substrate? Alternative: apparent growth only spends the initial internal packet.

Compare a stationary founder with maximal import/export and repair efforts in a finite local
source mixture against an otherwise identical source-free cell. Use chemistry seed 101, world
seed 101, a 16×16 periodic world, one founder at (8,8), 48 external material units in a normalized
sigma-two patch, and the declared default founder inventory/usable energy. No replenishment.
Start with the default resolved source pair. Both cases stop at 300 ticks, extinction, or a
30-second wall cap. Inspect each result before changing chemistry or adding population contests.

Predicted chain: local receptor levels → actual import → substrate-specific reactions → usable
energy after transport/maintenance → additional built matter and possibly division. Compare
imported chemical potential, reaction heat, constructed material, stored energy, damage, deaths
and per-species transfers. The decisive comparison is acquisition-funded growth beyond the
source-free control. A bookkeeping pass or population count alone is insufficient.

If the pair fails, identify the binding physical constraint from fluxes before retuning. A
source composition change must name the physical property criterion; a kinetic change must
name the affected rate and competing explanation. Register the specific comparison before
running it. Do not extend the horizon or launch seed searches automatically.

## Initial nutrition finding and registered correction

Ledger rows 3480–3481, `harness/artifacts/digital-chemistry/nutrition-initial/`: the empty
control survived 300 ticks and built 0.3165 material from its initial packet. The supplied cell
died from injury at tick 293, imported only 0.3001 material and built 0.3265. Its repair removed
0.4667 injury while total incurred injury was 1.4667. It spent the whole observation at least
20% below clear-medium mobility. These results fail the intended bootstrap opportunity.

The original stress surface couples the usable, rapidly diffusing corner to high stress for
chemistry seed 101. The bootstrap source rule consequently chooses IDs 15 and 10 near the
high-impedance, slow-diffusion edge. Both damage and restricted supply are plausible constraints.
The issue lies in joint physical opportunities, not a missing automatic catabolic path.

Registered correction: keep the same sixteen-term smooth basis, ranges, affinity and kinetics;
replace the broad stress saddle with one smooth central stress neighborhood,
`(1-cos(2πx/15))*(1-cos(2πy/15))/4`, plus the existing small seeded coefficient noise.
This candidate failed the existing slope check in all eight seeds: maximum adjacent normalized
stress steps were 0.2036–0.2112 against the 0.15 limit. It was rejected before cell assays.
Use the broader additive central surface
`0.5-0.25*cos(2πx/15)-0.25*cos(2πy/15)` instead, with the same noise. Recheck all eight seeds
and the unchanged slope/coverage limits. This preserves low-stress corners and a stressful
interior without the product candidate's steep edges.

First repeat the empty/finite pair with source IDs explicitly fixed to 15 and 10, isolating
the stress-surface change. Then inspect the resulting default source choice under the existing
physical selection rule. If that choice differs, run one empty/finite pair with that resolved
choice, identifying this as a bootstrap-composition comparison. Same 300 ticks, 30 seconds per
case, seed 101 and initial packets; four additional cases maximum. No mutation or longer runs.

The additive surface passed all 31 numerical/lifecycle checks. Fixed-source rows 3482–3483
still ended in extinction (ticks 297 and 274). Reapplying the unchanged source criterion selects
IDs 0 and 15. Rows 3484–3485 both reached 300 ticks; external supply funded 0.5979 construction
and 1.5205 imported matter, but final damage was 0.9984. The supplied cell incurred 1.4765 damage
and repaired 0.4781. Growth exists, but this is an inadequate bootstrap calibration.

Registered kinetic comparison: hold the new surface, source pair and all other laws fixed;
compare maximum injury rate 0.01 to the original 0.04. Current maximum repair is 0.008, so
saturating exposure remains harmful even with full repair. The competing explanation is that
better acquisition merely delays self-injury from internal/excreted products. Run empty/finite
pairs at damage rates 0.0075, 0.01 and 0.0125 (six cases total), preserving 300 ticks, 30 seconds,
seed 101 and the same packets. Inspect actual construction, terminal injury, repair expense and
energy. This tests a neighborhood of one kinetic choice; it does not establish sustained survival
or authorize a longer run. Differential exposure and paid detoxification still require their
separate causal tests before accepting the stress calibration.

Rows 3486–3491 completed all six cases. At rates 0.0075/0.01/0.0125, supplied construction was
0.6068/0.6023/0.5980 and terminal injury was 0/0.0010/0.0916; empty construction was
0.3196/0.3196/0.3184. Select 0.01 provisionally: acquisition-funded construction survives the

### Cross-feeding result

Rows 3492–3494 completed at 300 ticks. In the coupled case the donor produced 5.6250 ID128,
exported 4.7555, and the recipient imported 0.5457 and transformed 0.5282 to ID240. Recipient
construction was 0.4448 and it survived. Export-off and recipient-processing-off controls built
0.3960/0.4088 and their recipients died. Small off-target processing was measured explicitly;
it did not sustain either control. Maximum energy residual was below 6e-13%.

Export was expensive for the donor: construction was 1.1057 with export versus 6.6719 without,
and donor descendants numbered 1 versus 4. This establishes a source-powered cross-feeding
opportunity with donor cost, not mutual benefit, evolved cooperation or stable coexistence.

### Exposure and detoxification result

Rows 3495–3500 reached 300 ticks. Compatible versus distant membranes incurred 0.1754/0.2595
damage under external dosing and 0.1299/0.2004 with internal dosing. Compatibility reduced
exposure without eliminating injury. Background products and paid repair remain active, so these
are whole-cell results rather than isolated susceptibility ratios.

The detoxifier imported 0.1059 ID120 and transformed 0.0682 to ID240. Its injury was 0.4385 versus
0.4442 for the self-reaction control, and repair energy was 1.1652 versus 1.3352. Construction
was 0.8949 versus 0.8796; both survived. The chemical transformation and reduced repair burden
are demonstrated, with a small construction advantage in this fixture. No large protective or
reproductive advantage is claimed. The active case took 15.2 seconds, which also flags field and
reaction cost for the required operational measurements.
