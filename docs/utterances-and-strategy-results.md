## Work and reach calibration

`q = 0.0002 work/area`, the existing actuator rate `p = 0.2 work/(material·second)`,
`tau = 0.8 seconds` and newborn core area `A0 = 0.25` imply origination cost 0.00005.
Ordinary mouth stock 0.04 enables at most 0.0064 work, radius 3.18, before reservations.
Ordinary ear stock 0.01 gives gain 0.5 when undamaged. A second listener costs the speaker
no extra debit and receives no usable work. Symbols have identical prices.

The legal core allele [-3,3] and mouth allele [-3,3] bound newborn mouth stock at
`4 × 0.04 × exp(3) = 3.214`, power-limited work 0.514 and radius 28.61. Automatic growth
up to twice genetic newborn biomass bounds the same genotype's work at 1.028 and radius 40.45.
Actual energy after the shared reserve can lower these bounds; the half-shorter-world guard
also applies. Thus the default world cannot become an affordable world-wide broadcast under
ordinary genetic growth. Explicit diagnostic biomass grants can exceed ordinary growth bounds.

Speech runs after genuine reflex evaluations and current motor debits, before movement. It
protects ordinary upcoming maintenance, held motors and both learning layers through the shared
interval reserve, uses no prospective chemical income and dissipates its debit exactly once.
Subsequent growth reads the remaining account. Sparse dyadic event membership skips silent
space; dense same-disk speech can still require quadratic candidate encounters.

## Findings

Eighteen focused checks cover carrier, strategy and consumer integration. The 96-tick probe
recorded context +0.845/−0.239/+0.342 at ticks 32/64/96. Reflex swimming begins after the first
context publication and stops after the second: effort is positive at tick 36 and zero at tick
68. At tick 96 displacement is 5.25 world units and cumulative motor work is 0.1002. This is a
funded residence/dispersal switch in an authored single-cell fixture, with no measured ecological
return. Contact readings use the preceding display regardless of cell order. Selected observers
do not alter checkpoints, and context ablation persists locally without changing other cells.

The initial cost panel completed all horizons: ledger rows 4433–4435, local directory
`frontend/harness/artifacts/utterance-strategy-cost-2026-10-01`. Serial WASM measured 11.88,
9.79 and 1.15 ticks/second for silent, sparse and dense conditions. These ran while local CI was
active and are operating samples, not clean comparative timing or native multicore predictions.
Silent emitted zero events; sparse emitted 620 and had zero recipients; dense emitted 20,000
and delivered 39,637,305 listener events. All retained 2,000 living cells. Dense contact and
chatter share the measured cost, so their contributions are not causally separated. No delivery
quota or range change was introduced. Checkpoints were 77.4–77.9 MiB; serial WASM linear memory
high-water reached 1.20 GiB including original and restored fixtures. The benchmark's dense
256-channel field is a constructed load, not a prediction of the continuing world's storage.

Immediate checkpoint roundtrips were exact; future warm-cache trajectories differed after
restore, as allowed by the existing derived-cache contract. Both continuations passed material
and work closure.

The registered paired follow-up completed 36 measured ticks: ledger row 4436, local directory
`frontend/harness/artifacts/utterance-strategy-paired-2026-10-01`. It recorded 620 utterances and
497 listener arrivals, with 2,000 surviving cells and 12.64 ticks/second. Ordinary motion can
separate the initially adjacent pairs; receipts are cumulative arrivals, not a reception
percentage or a measure of controller benefit. Checkpoints occupied 77.90 MiB, and WASM memory
high-water was 844.63 MiB including the restored fixture. Both continuation accounts closed.
The first sparse fixture's zero arrivals remain recorded. These short operating samples miss
the approximate 30-tick reference; performance remains a low-priority operating limit.

The complete Rust kernel suite passes 387 checks; native server checks pass 17 with one
