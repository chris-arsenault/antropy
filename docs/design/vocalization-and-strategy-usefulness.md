## Delivered paths and limits

Own paid uptake history can hold useful activity through a finite interruption after its present
external cue disappears. The M2 matched comparison captures 0.272136 with context versus
0.000324 without it, above whole-window strategic upkeep. Learning is static in this diagnostic:
it establishes a priced memory opportunity, not learned anticipation. Independent reflex and
strategic learning switches expose separate actual paid counters; context and gain can be held
out without disabling slow inference. Brief and sustained changes also preserve affordable
metabolic alternatives; neither migration nor new colony founding is inferred.

In the M6 ordinary-World diagnostic, a producer's private feedstock cue changes its paid byte.
The receiver's ordinary reflex uses a signed bit to change uptake, converts the producer's
intermediate and exports a usable product. Correctly associated content gives receiver import136
0.047903 and export128 0.049918, against 0.001090 and 0.002971 for the wrong association.
The speaker imports128 0.014282 versus 0.000129, and ends with reserve 0.084034 versus 0.067786.
Withheld-content results remain near the wrong association. Total speech costs 0.126237 versus
0.113437: initial event expense is matched, then the actual feedback loop funds one additional
event. This is a physical sender-return path without assigned rewards or sender identity.
It does not establish an evolved convention or indefinitely sustainable cooperation.
Silence ends with speaker reserve 0.144935 and capture 1.935118, versus informative reserve
0.084034 and capture 1.925281. Thus useful content relative to paid wrong content does not
repay the full speech investment here. The selected ordinary carrier has a physical return
route, but a sustainable sender incentive remains unestablished; do not call this cooperative profit.

Live `speech` and `listening` independently gate event production and reception. `hearingContent`
and `hearingBearing` independently withhold signed content and directional moments while retaining
paid events and body investment. Activity remains when content is withheld; byte zero remains
an event. Startup `features.vocalization=false` still removes both organs, whereas runtime flags
retain funded bodies and their upkeep. Existing interference, periodic geometry, once-only
continuation and newborn reset tests remain authoritative. No larger alphabet, relay, trust label
or hidden identity was introduced. Diagnostic responses are not ordinary founder policies.

## Purpose

Make paid vocalization and slow strategic control useful sources of conditional behavior.
Vocalization should offer information another cell can use beyond its existing local cues;
strategy should use history and local rhythms to change decisions in ways an immediate reflex
cannot achieve as economically. Their usefulness must be considered separately and together:
hearing may influence reflexes directly, and strategy may help without communication.

Possible opportunities include responding to changing supply, deciding when to remain or
depart, coordinating activity through local information and carrying useful experience across
births. These are questions, not seeded policies, assigned meanings or required outcomes.

## Why this direction is open

The [v49 delivery checks](../utterances-and-strategy-results.md) established paid byte delivery,
inherited context and an authored history/context/action path. They did not measure ecological
return. The October 2 overnight v50 review found frequent paid utterances and hearing events,
alongside varying strategic context and substantial combined controller-learning expenditure.
Those observations did not identify useful message content, improved listener decisions,
sender return or a benefit attributable to strategic history or learning.

Activity alone is underwhelming evidence of usefulness. It also does not establish that these
features are useless: combined learning cost is not a strategic-layer cost estimate, and no
matched comparison isolated either feature's return. Earlier detailed controller records were
not retained throughout the night. Local provenance is
`frontend/harness/artifacts/mortality-overnight-20261002/`, ticks 87,379–476,061;
the detailed surviving inspection is at tick 476,061.

## Corrections that change the evidence baseline

The overnight review predates the [finite binding partition](../key-binding-correction.md) and
[v54 reservoir correction](mortality-recycling.md#october-12-correction-finite-contents-and-external-supply).
Changing chemical access or preventing a trace deposit from rewriting future food can improve
survival without improving communication or memory. The review did not isolate a speech,
history or learning defect; its activity and combined-cost findings remain historical evidence,
not a diagnosis that one of those processes is broken.

The [actuator-feedback correction](decisions-and-evidence.md#review-corrections-and-actuator-feedback)
already supplies realized execution feedback to the reflex layer. That does not supply the
marginal value of an action, meaningful message content or a useful history timescale. A later
comparison should distinguish these remaining relationships from the corrected stock-input defect.

The [run-start ablations](../exploration-ablation.md) now permit separate vocalization and
strategy interventions with effects and costs removed. The October 12–13 recovery observation
disabled both, so its population improvement establishes neither their benefit nor their harm.
The information advantage, sender return and paid value of strategic history remain open.

## Requirements retained for continued investigation

- Identify information a speaker has that a listener lacks, when that information remains
  relevant at reception and which ordinary paid decision it could improve. Distinguish useful
  content and bearing from redundant cues, interference and indiscriminate chatter.
- Explain a plausible sender or descendant return as well as listener benefit. Consider
  silence and eavesdropping as valid choices; do not add cooperation rewards or kin rules.
- Identify decisions that need history, anticipation or persistent context. Specify the
  relationship among strategic timescale, local environmental rhythms, inherited memory,
  learning and reflex actions rather than treating nonzero context as useful strategy.
- Examine whether the current input representation, message superposition, context-to-reflex
  connection and shared mutation/learning law permit accessible useful changes. Select any
  representation or coupling revision from the missing opportunity, not network size alone.
- Account separately for speech, hearing capacities, strategic upkeep and both learning
  layers. Specify the chosen shared operations, durable state, ordinary configuration and
  bounded observations needed to connect readings and context to actions and returns.

## Boundaries and completion

Keep local paid information, funded mouth/ear capacities and ordinary neural action selection.
Messages carry no usable work, material, sender identity or predefined meaning. Controllers
receive no global population state, coordinates, destinations, future refill draws or fitness
score. Preserve the shared mathematical architecture, controller boundary, birth-local
inheritance and Rust-owned physical state. Reuse existing observations and diagnostic runners.

Complete the design by choosing the information, memory and action relationships and explaining
how their conditional value can plausibly exceed their costs. Later bounded comparisons can
separate vocalization, strategic context and learning where that distinction changes a design
decision. Working implementation and plausible intended effects suffice for delivery; evolved
language, cooperation or long-term strategic diversity are not release gates. No new experiment
campaign is selected by this direction.
