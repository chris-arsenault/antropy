## Reporting decision

Read the Canonry event-log/Monte Carlo contracts, primitive schema registry and TelemetryBatch
implementation. Retain their separation of typed physical facts, observation projections and
analytical queries. Do not copy their event bus, code generator, export worker or broad table set.

The bacterial kernel emits optional typed resource-flow facts at the resolver that owns each
change. A harness-owned observer aggregates exact flows in 100-tick organism windows and records
lifecycles and sampled state. Observers receive scalar facts, never mutable world references.
No event callback feeds controller decisions, changes RNG or enters checkpoints. Disabled observers
have no durable state. Bounded tests compare complete checkpoints with and without observation and
reconcile flow totals to existing ledgers.

Generated JSONL tables carry explicit schemas and are loaded into a per-study DuckDB file by a
small local importer. The existing SQLite ledger retains run identity and results. This uses the
available DuckDB Python installation, with no hosted backend or service. Raw dimensional quantities
remain queryable; reports use percentages of world area, organism-time and resource expenditure,
with explicit denominators. Incomplete runs remain labeled incomplete. Recent windows avoid
comparing cumulative production with standing field area.

## Genotype finding before causal runs

All 78 living lineage-18 members carry RNN weight 1574 = -0.07692401111125946; the founder has
zero there. With the current layout this is hidden unit 14 → toxin output. Founder hidden unit 14
reads the previous task byte. The negative connection therefore gates down toxin production when
that unit is active. It first appears in genotype 54, organism 101, at tick 331. Founder 18's other
daughter, organism 102, lacked it; its two daughters died without further divisions. Later mutations
and shared spatial history remain confounders. Output weights are outside inherited Hebbian trace
assimilation, so this particular difference is an ordinary mutation, not acquired-weight transfer.

Leading causal prediction: the shared toxin gate lowers toxin spending and self-exposure, increasing
the nutrient fraction available for construction. Reverting that one connection should remove
at least part of the benefit. Lower damage with the same food intake is distinct from finding more
food. The competing explanation is that this mutation hitchhiked with favorable placement.

is not the supported explanation here. This does not exclude subtler path or physical effects in
the multi-mutation genomes. The single-locus contests hold physical genes constant.

Matrix remains spatially sparse: the retained-community mean half-speed coverage is 0.090% of world
area, with a 0.313% peak. Yet about 8% of organism-time encounters ≥20% matrix slowing. Food, cells
and matrix are spatially clustered. Coverage and affected organism-time answer different questions;
neither cumulative production nor a single standing-area endpoint establishes ecological importance.
Neutral signaling remains explicitly disabled. Near-zero energy/material residuals are accounting
closure, not dormant activity. Repair can matter without directly lethal toxin exposure.

The long controls remain secondary evidence: at tick 50,000 the full/frozen seed-101 leading shares
are 22.06%/11.20%, and seed-102 leading shares are 14.00%/11.19%. Frozen seed 101 loses lineage 18;
its corresponding full run retains it. The dominant family differs between seeds. These results do
not reconstruct the user's 57.35% takeover, nor do they establish that inherited learning caused it.

- The observer is outside durable simulation state and has no control feedback. Full bounded CI

- Two held-out environment seeds and one historical community are limited replication. The tests do
  not establish coexistence, invasion from rarity, adaptive physical specialization, useful learning
  inheritance or separately beneficial offensive secretion. Reversion covers one observed background.

The next recommendation is a small physical-effect comparison: retain secretion cost but remove
toxin injury in the single-allele contest, then test mixture frequency if that result warrants it.
This separates saving precursor from escaping toxin pressure before designing additional competition.
Do not automatically add family chemistry, new food systems or another optimizer. The current result
is real adaptation toward lower waste, while the desired range of strategic alternatives remains open.
