# Numerical engine measurements

**Historical measurements.** The September15 fresh core has a separate
[registration and result record](rebuild-results.md). The binaries, checkpoints and claims below
retain their original scope and do not certify the new production laws.

September 14, 2026. These are bounded mechanism and computation checks for the replacement
Rust/WASM engine. They do not demonstrate evolved adaptation, persistent coexistence or
days/weeks endurance. The browser and harness now use this sole Rust/WASM runtime.

The later [reliability results](reliability-results.md) govern current schema-11 machinery,
chemistry-version-3 opportunities and actual browser limits. The figures below remain evidence
for their named binaries; their former v10 continuation claim does not authorize loading old saves.

checks. The [diagnostic continuity record](diagnostic-continuity.md#verification-and-operating-cost)

## Nutrition and numerical sensitivity

Ledger 3625–3635; [retained case reports](../../evidence/digital-chemistry/numerical-nutrition/),
full artifacts in `frontend/harness/artifacts/numerical-nutrition-01/`. A stationary diagnostic
cell receives either no external supply or 48 units of the ordinary source pair in a finite
sigma-two patch. Mutation and learning are frozen. At 300 ticks, the supplied case constructs
4.087/4.120/4.138 units with physiology intervals 0.8/0.4/0.2, and divides once in every arm.
The empty controls construct 0.402/0.402/0.397 from their initial reserves and never divide.
The separate empty-reserve check ends in starvation at tick 388.

Mesh 1 at interval 0.8 constructs 3.445 units with supply and also divides once. Its roughly
16% lower construction is a material numerical sensitivity; the direction of the resource
benefit survives refinement. These results support the initial coarse model, not accurate
microscopic concentrations. The ordinary moving controller survives and divides with supply;
the empty moving control dies at tick 288. No colonization or long-term survival claim follows.

## Chemical opportunities

Ledger 3642–3657; [retained case reports](../../evidence/digital-chemistry/numerical-opportunities/),
full artifacts in `frontend/harness/artifacts/numerical-opportunities-01/`. Cases isolate
individual chemistry by preventing division, with explicit common starting stocks, no mutation
or learning, ordinary diagnostic RNNs, and 300-tick horizons (emission: 100). Cumulative chemical
flows are measured separately from standing inventories. Samples named `lastLiving` precede
death when a cell dies; they are not final living-cell observations.

- **Cross-feeding:** the donor exports 4.900 units of ID128; the recipient imports 0.356 and
  converts 0.342 to ID240, remaining alive at tick 300. The recipient dies in both donor-export-off
  and recipient-processing-off controls. This establishes a costly metabolic dependency,
  not evolved cooperation or mutual benefit.
- **Emission:** donor export produces receiver receptor input 0.106 and connected import effort
  0.308 before the receiver dies. The export-off control has zero input and effort. Detectability
  and neural response exist; the fixture does not sustain the receiver or establish useful communication.
- **Compatibility:** external dosing incurs damage 0.0197 with a matching membrane versus 0.1507
  with the distant membrane. Internal dosing incurs 0.0285 versus 0.1922; the distant arm dies.
  Matching susceptibility remains nonzero. Exposure and paid repair are recorded, so survival
  alone is not the evidence.
- **Detoxification:** the transformer consumes 0.269 units of ID120. Injury is 0.152 versus
  0.274 and repair expenditure 0.350 versus 0.698 for the zero-offset control. Construction is
  slightly lower with conversion, 0.611 versus 0.631. Reduced injury does not automatically
  confer a growth advantage.
- **Corpse capture:** uptake captures 0.0476 units of ID128 and converts 0.0440. Construction is
  0.1446 versus 0.1401 without uptake; starvation occurs at tick 88 versus 56. This is additional
  access to finite resources, not predation or indefinite survival.
- **Paid impedance:** ordinary metabolism/export accumulates 1.171 units of high-impedance,
  low-diffusion ID15 by tick 300. The stopped-export movement follow-up has a modest effect:
  travel is 14.122 versus 14.254 units after removing ID15, at equal motor expenditure 0.4732.
  Initial mobility is 0.926 versus 0.996. This fixture does not make an impermeable barrier.
- **Degradation:** the transformer consumes 0.0528 units of ID15. After ordinary death it returns
  0.00745, versus 0.05865 in the zero-offset control. Transformation prevents most captured
  barrier material from returning; a competitive payoff remains unproved.

The barrier/death follow-ups are ledger 3658–3662, in
`frontend/harness/artifacts/numerical-barrier-01/`; [retained diffusion/death reports](../../evidence/digital-chemistry/numerical-barrier/).
With the paid deposit retained, a tracer's
second spatial moment is 4.863 after two model seconds, versus 5.733 with ID15 removed.
Pulse/background subtraction retains approximately 0.999001 tracer material in both cases.
Thus this deposit also impedes diffusion, separately from its modest effect on one cell's travel.

## Ordinary startup

Ledger 3663; [retained report](../../evidence/digital-chemistry/numerical-startup.json),
full artifacts in `frontend/harness/artifacts/numerical-startup-01/`. The actual default world starts
with 48 founders in two colonies and reaches tick 600 in 2.35 seconds. It imports 36.63 material,
constructs 46.91, completes one division and records 13 deaths, leaving 36 cells. Forty-six
division attempts are blocked by local placement. Material/energy residuals are below 4e-10.

This is evidence that source access, paid growth, reproduction, crowding and mortality reach
the production population. It is not sufficient evidence for ecological persistence, diversity,
adaptation or acceptable visible motion. Worker integration and consumer migration are implemented;
recovery under actual browser conditions and human visual review remain unverified.

## Accumulated-history storage

Ledger 3664–3665; `frontend/harness/artifacts/numerical-storage-01/` and
`numerical-storage-02/`. Seven bounded cases cover 100,000 and two million synthetic
ancestor records, empty/patchy/widespread/dense chemical fields, and the ordinary
48-founder world. Every case preserves the browser observation package and exact
physical continuation for three paired steps after five measured steps.

The first two-million-record case exposed a 906 MiB WASM memory high-water mark.
Compact 56-byte ancestor records, single-buffer snapshot encoding and releasing the
previous ABI reply reduced the same sequential workload to 406 MiB (55% less).
These figures include simultaneous original/restored worlds and temporary snapshots;
they are allocated WASM memory, not total browser memory or steady live-world usage.
The large checkpoint remains 68,114,072 raw bytes and 11,062,184 compressed bytes.
The v10 wire format is unchanged. Archived pre-change checkpoints for the large
history and default world restore byte-for-byte and continue identically for three
steps under both archived and current engines. The compatibility check ran alongside
the second storage panel, so its timings are not an isolated speed comparison.

This is storage and continuation evidence, not a days-long ecological run. The
configured ancestry limit still pauses the simulation when reached; no history is
silently discarded.

Ledger 3675 repeats all seven cases after strict body/history validation and durable intervention
retention were added. Every case still preserves exact physical and observational continuation.
The two-million-record case remains at 406 MiB high-water memory, with 250 ms serialization,
827 ms compression and 421 ms decompression/restore. The default checkpoint takes 19/76/94 ms
respectively. These are local sequential measurements, not browser responsiveness guarantees.
The [retained report](../../evidence/digital-chemistry/numerical-storage.json) includes engine
digests, all case timings and stopping status; full artifacts are in
`frontend/harness/artifacts/numerical-storage-03/`.
