## Outcome and controlling corrections

Identify and repair what prevents cells from creating and exploiting a sustaining chemical
economy beyond their starting inputs. The user identifies two acceptable ecological paths:

- A significant portion of cells sustains itself on other cells' waste.
- Cells modify reservoirs so their actual supply changes from the initial chemistry, and cells
  sustain themselves on that changed supply.

Examine both paths; the outcome does not require both to emerge. Do not assign chemical roles,
seed a specialist population as the solution, or prescribe a community composition.
“Nonstarting” is relative to the resolved world's initial supply **and** finite founder/priming
chemistry. Reusing an initially primed circuit member is not departure from that initial economy.
For the current 32/168 world, retain the full starting set 32, 160, 168 and 40 when classifying it.

The user's November 3 correction controls this plan: explain and address why these strategies
do not become sustaining alternatives. A new monitoring exercise is not the requested repair.
Different reaction products, greater conversion volume, nonstarting imports, total population
and survival alone are not evidence of the intended ecological outcome. Better extraction from
the original food can be beneficial without accomplishing this goal.

transport → conversion → all physical expenses → growth and replacement → altered local supply.
For a failed link, identify its implementation owner, isolate the cause and repair it, then
recalculate downstream consequences. Do not select a new mechanism from an attractive name.

Use the [shared mathematical foundation](../design/chemistry/computational-foundation.md),

Any change must state the shared state and operation, units, material/work bounds and cost.
No chemical-specific rewards, waste-consumer mutation, fallback controller, free work, parental
function buffer, machinery construction or hidden environmental rescue is allowed.

Use the existing [resource-economy method](../design/chemistry/resource-economy.md) before stepping.
Its current `engine/src/economy.rs` calculation uses imported proportions for retained material
and age-zero maintenance; it excludes several live expenses and losses. Extend only the missing
calculation needed for a repair decision, using production operators. A positive ceiling is not
a sustaining consumer. Examine actual retained mixtures, age, light, passive exchange, injury,
repair, learning, growth and division costs in neighborhood continuations.

Bound diagnostic work by the decision, modeled renewal/reserve times and wall cost; do not bake
in a tick target. Use existing checkpoints, intervention controls and local harness artifacts.
No seed sweep, horizon extension or search for an impressive endpoint replaces a causal result.
Handcrafted genotypes/controllers may isolate a physical link; label them diagnostic and do not
deliver them as evolved strategies. Keep all raw artifacts and the ledger ignored and local.

## 1. Repair secondary-substrate viability

**Change to deliver:** correct the shared physical constraints that prevent available
nonstarting material from sustaining a consumer, where those constraints are demonstrated.

Start with the current world's actual waste, local concentrations and cell physiology. Price
the complete material and work budget for a consumer and the producer supplying it. Determine
whether useful substrate survives local loss and competition, can enter at a sufficient rate,
and leaves enough return after conversion and expenses to fund continued growth and replacement.
Distinguish an energetically exhausted end product from an intermediate with remaining usable
potential, and distinguish both from material made useful by accounted external work.

Compare the ordinary consumer, a diagnostic viable candidate and a control lacking the relevant
uptake or conversion in the same captured neighborhood. Preserve initial reserves, light,
source clocks and competing cells. Follow repeated operation rather than spending a starting
energy packet once. Where initial chemistry contributes, account for its contribution explicitly.

If no candidate can pay under the local supply, identify the governing limitation before changing
it: exhausted potential, inaccessible external drive, delivery/retention, binding-capacity
dilution, inhibition, susceptibility or competing expenses. Implement the shared-law correction
that addresses the demonstrated cause. If viable consumers already work, preserve the law and
carry the accessibility or competition barrier into phase 3. Do not make output look nutritious
by granting work or waiving costs.

**Embedded checks:** actual nonstarting uptake/conversion supports paid maintenance and biomass
replacement; removing the responsible process removes the benefit under matched conditions.
For waste reuse, isolate producer-derived availability from ordinary abiotic production and
death spills. Preserve material/work accounts and ordinary production-founder viability.

## 2. Repair cell-driven reservoir feedback

**Change to deliver:** repair the path by which cell activity changes usable reservoir supply,
where the existing coupling cannot produce a consequential benefit.

Trace cell material and paid actions through local interaction/light signals into reservoir
conversion, held inventory, future-supply composition, refill, release and cell intake. Start
with `engine/src/source_medium.rs`, `source_lifecycle.rs`, the source conversion owner and shared
public chemistry. Check actual magnitudes against source turnover and living-cell costs.

Separate three mechanisms: transient mixing of recovered material into held stock; public
conversion of that stock; and evolution of the external boundary used for future imports.
Read released mixtures, not just normalized profiles. A changed reservoir that supplies unusable
material is not advantageous environmental modification.

Use matched local continuations to separate cell-caused changes from reservoir self-influence,
abiotic weathering and initial conditions. Isolate the responsible chemical or optical
contribution while retaining the other physical effects needed by the comparison. Removing all
cells or all food is not by itself an interpretable test of cell-driven chemical influence.

If the feedback is absent, too weak, self-dominated or cannot return a benefit, repair the shared
coupling and its physical cost. Derive the required influence and timescale from ordinary cell
and source budgets. Do not add a reservoir occupation bonus, special conversion destination,
unbounded persistent mark or a trace-deposit assignment to all future imports. Check who
receives the benefit: the modifier, descendants or other local consumers. A costly effect
captured entirely by competitors may be an evolutionary barrier rather than missing conversion.

**Embedded checks:** cell activity causes changed actual release, that nonstarting release
supports beneficiary growth/replacement, and a matched contribution control removes the causal
link. Include ordinary refill and empty/full-source behavior; retain the no-amplification,
conservation and work-account regressions.

## 3. Repair evolutionary access and selection

**Change to deliver:** repair the demonstrated barriers between a viable physical strategy and
its acquisition, expression and retention through ordinary evolution.

For the viable strategies identified above, trace the genetic and behavioral changes required
from actual cells. Examine transporter/enzyme alignment, membrane susceptibility, body allocation,
program duplication and transformed products, conditional activity and inherited learning.
Use the production mutation operator to distinguish proposals that never occur, variants that
are born but fail to express, and variants that express but lose their ecological advantage.
Existing broad mutation tails alone do not show that a viable combination is accessible.

Test intermediate changes as well as a complete diagnostic consumer. Identify whether uptake,
conversion and control require an unsupported simultaneous jump or destroy the parent's viable
function before secondary use can pay. Do not restore parental-function buffers or directed
mutation. Fix a representation or shared-operator defect if demonstrated; do not increase mutation
rates or suppress evolution merely to obtain a longer-lived population.

Compare returns under actual primary-food competition, local waste availability and producer
turnover. Determine why secondary use would pay rather than merely supplement abundant original
food. If no selective advantage exists, repair the shared physical tradeoff creating that absence;
do not force a niche by starving all founders, rewarding novelty or imposing species quotas.
Evaluate broad recognition, specialization and environmental modification through the same
finite capacities and expenses. Conditional advantages must come from physical circumstances.

**Embedded checks:** the relevant inherited changes can be produced and expressed by ordinary
birth operations, retain funded viability, and improve growth/replacement in the conditions
where the alternative resource exists. Controls distinguish resource benefit from founder
reserves, generic cost reduction and unrelated mutations. A diagnostic endpoint or single
beneficial locus is not evidence that an ordinary population has established a secondary economy.

## Execution decisions and reassessment

November 3 execution identifies unintended coupling between recognition preference and binding
strength. A preference normalization preserves total binding strength at fixed bias while still
dividing finite capacity across recognized chemicals. Founder profiles are unchanged. The
`bindingIndependentStrength` startup switch retains the coupled law for explicit comparisons;
the repair is enabled in ordinary startup. This changes neither mutation input nor energy prices.

The waste-reuse comparison supports a sustaining consequence of this repair for a constructed
preference intermediate. The reservoir comparison isolates existing cell-caused changes to held
chemistry and future supply, but does not establish advantageous evolved reservoir modification.
Current reservoirs already supply substantial nonstarting material that cells poorly exploit.
Accordingly phase 2 retains the existing reservoir laws and the finite-deposit correction; no
additional reservoir feature is justified. The selected implementation addresses access to
secondary food, with waste reuse as the demonstrated sustaining path. This preserves the user's
either-path outcome; it does not relabel reservoir profile changes as ecological success.

The implementing agent owns causal diagnosis, formulas within the governing principles and
bounded diagnostic choices. No new mechanism is selected by this plan in advance of that work.
If existing laws already support an advantageous, reachable strategy, identify and address the
remaining selection barrier rather than rewriting those laws. If a result contradicts the
approach, update the affected phases before proceeding; branch concrete multi-step prerequisites
with `sulion plan branch` and return when repaired.
