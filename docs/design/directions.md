# Design directions

**Status:** Current reference — registry of every design direction with its status and owning document.

Each direction appears once, under its present status. Versions are physical checkpoint formats;
dates are 2026. The owning document holds the design or execution record; the decision column
links the reasoning, rejections and evidence. Current equations belong to the
[composed laws](chemistry/composed-runtime.md); priorities belong to the [work order](README.md);
unselected work belongs to the [backlog](../backlog.md); plans are listed in the
[plan index](../plans/README.md).

## Corrected mechanisms and evidence baselines — October 13

Several directions began with an apparently ineffective process, then exposed a defect in
that process or its experimental conditions. Carry the correction into the research question.
Keep negative results with their recorded law, configuration and source revision; do not
present them as fresh failures of the corrected mechanism. Checkpoint format alone does not
identify the law: the October 6 binding correction retained the physical layout.

| Process and fixed defect | Corrected baseline | Consequence for the direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mortality recovery could replace every future refill's chemistry with a trace corpse deposit. | V54, `c8404bd`: recovery changes finite contents; the external boundary profile evolves separately and owns no stock. [Correction](mortality-recycling.md#october-12-correction-finite-contents-and-external-supply). | Supply-profile amplification is fixed. [Local feeding](local-mortality-feeding.md) still asks about global masking, eligibility, timing and survivor return; early improvement does not establish long-term rescue. |
| Reducing sourceRate also halved startup batches and reservoir-derived priming. | `573b95e`: rate 0.1, lifetime 1200 retains full batches; gap 600 and recovery response remain unchanged. [Startup correction](../mortality-startup-correction.md). | The first v50 extinction does not isolate recovery's effect. Distinguish establishment resources from recycling, discharge ceilings and later replacement. |
| Independent sigmoid keys allowed positive bias to grant near-universal recognition and protection. | `0e9ecee`: one competing partition, including an unbound state, limits total affinity to one per site. [Binding correction](../key-binding-correction.md). | Universal full-strength binding is a corrected model defect. [Chemical specialization](chemical-key-specialization.md) now asks whether finite recognition produces useful inherited differences; broad composed metabolism remains possible. |
| Body-stock inputs repeated biomass rather than reporting actuator effect. | V48, `ff175da`: accepted activity relative to full-effort capacity replaces redundant stock inputs. [Decision](decisions-and-evidence.md#review-corrections-and-actuator-feedback). | Conditional allocation and optical effort have execution feedback. Activity still does not measure marginal benefit; missing payoff is not automatically missing feedback. |
| Dissolved slope transport compounded concentration ratios with mesh face count. | `e754743`: the shared face coefficient follows net exchange direction. [Decision](decisions-and-evidence.md#review-corrections-and-actuator-feedback). | Earlier strong ridge-to-hollow pooling was a discretization defect, not evidence of useful terrain-mediated chemistry or a required environmental force. |
| Partial harness configurations silently inherited ecology terrain, seasons and source placement. | `7ab52b8`: each world names a preset or supplies a complete configuration. [Decision](decisions-and-evidence.md#review-corrections-and-actuator-feedback). | Use explicit physical conditions before attributing a failed opportunity to controller or chemical incapacity. |

Light and vocalization/strategy usefulness remain open; these corrections do not establish
their return. The October 12–13 recovery observation used corrected keys and enabled recovery
with photoreception, cover, emission, vocalization and strategy disabled. Its population cannot
answer those features' usefulness questions. Owning directions retain the remaining alternatives.
