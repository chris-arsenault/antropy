## Original decision record

The following September 15 proposal and rationale remain provenance. Its prescriptions for
thermodynamic equations and its instruction to defer documentation reconciliation are superseded
by the user's later design clarification and explicit documentation request.

### Context

Antropy's intended use is continuing observation of a sparse spatial ecosystem in which inherited
differences have physical consequences. The user accepted discrete elements, fixed heritable
machinery and local genome/RNN cells while authorizing coherent changes to their implementation.
The physical system must support useful movement, accessible variation, local environmental
feedback, conserved material, funded life cycles and a usable complete browser workload.

Three proposals emphasized different parts of that system: thermodynamic driving forces,
finite machinery engagement and evolutionary accessibility, then physical field coupling. The
user requested one integrated design that preserves all earlier feedback without promoting the
latest example into the principal requirement. Their September 15 confirmation authorizes
preserving that integrated direction and preparing the feature-start plan.

### Decision

Use four connected laws over common material state: energetic driving forces, mobility through
the medium, finite machinery engagement and transformation kinetics. A shared free-energy model
supplies chemical potentials, passive cell forces and reaction/exchange driving forces. Explicit
material and energy/reservoir accounts cover metabolism, physical rearrangement, assembly, repair,
remodeling, death and external sources/sinks.

Retain 256 discrete elements on a smooth bounded 16 × 16 manifold. Generate validated intrinsic
properties and compact interaction profiles. Local mixtures and embodied cell interfaces source
shared finite-range interaction fields; symmetric energetic coupling and bounded crowding govern
passive responses. Mobility represents resistance separately from potential gradients.

Use small fixed heritable machinery arrays, initially four receptors, four transporters and four
unary enzymes, with inherited membrane compatibility and funded investment. Shared engagement
allocates finite capacity. Transformation families overlap continuously across discrete products;
activation barriers limit throughput for large chemical-space changes while respecting forward
and reverse energetic coupling. Installed function changes through bounded, gradual paid
remodeling and actual-stock inheritance.

Maintain individual local RNN control, generic paid biomass, explicit internal/external stress,
private learning and birth-local assimilation. Finite uneven resource opportunities, common slow
washout, useful movement and at least two starting colonies establish spatial operating conditions.
The ordinary startup workload remains 48 founders. Ecological meanings arise from use, with
conditional opportunities established through analysis and small probes.

Retain the sole Rust/WASM kernel and same-worker borrowed rendering. Extend compiled operators,
matrix reductions, bounded observations and exact current-schema recovery together. The complete
declared workload must reach at least 30 ticks/second while reporting model-time throughput.
Genealogy, multiscale spatial observation and actual physical/chemical inspection remain required.

The root plan owns implementation scope, milestones and evidence gates. Exact functions, field
rank, reference scales and numerical tolerances are implementer-owned selections validated within
this direction. Acceptance of architecture does not assert implementation, evolved diversity or
uninterrupted weeks of execution.

### Alternatives considered

- **Independent corrections to current formulas.** Existing linear energy, separate impedance
  responses and atomic refitting are inexpensive to modify individually. Retaining them as fixed
  boundaries would leave composition, forces, processing and inherited expression inconsistent.
  The selected work replaces their shared assumptions and consumers together.
- **Thermodynamics as the complete model.** Energy constraints help prevent impossible cycles,
  but they do not select reaction speeds, machinery capacity, injury rates or accessible genotype
  transitions. The four-law design keeps these responsibilities explicit and connected.
- **Finite engagement as the universal interaction.** Occupancy explains saturation and substrate
  competition economically. Alone it does not provide spatial chemical forces, a coherent movement
  law or transformation difficulty. It is one law within the physical system.
- **Field forces added alongside the existing economy.** This could provide visible attraction
  quickly, but cached reaction energies and separate uptake caps would leave work and delivery
  inconsistent. Fields and metabolism therefore share energetic state and near-cell transport.
- **Detailed molecular, electromagnetic or fluid reconstruction.** Such models offer established
  physical laws at additional state and computational cost. The user requested an artificial
  chemistry preserving discrete elements and genome/RNN organisms, with examples used to inform
  coupling. The selected finite-range continuum approximation serves that experimental identity.
- **Evolving variable genome structure immediately.** This expands possible complexity but also
  changes the genome architecture under investigation. Fixed slots give a bounded initial system
  whose physical chemistry and accessible mutations can be evaluated first.

### Consequences

The chemical definition, compiled genotype operators, installed state, integrator, work ledgers,
diagnostics and current checkpoint schema must change coherently. Existing source geometry,
allocation patterns, immutable genotype ownership, ancestry storage and observation transport
are reusable subject to source-level verification.

State-dependent chemical potentials cannot be cached as genotype constants. Explicit field
delivery needs one compatible membrane exchange approximation. Contact correction, fission,
remodeling, thermal motion and external insertion/removal must have consistent energetic meaning.
Nonlinear coupling adds stability and bandwidth risks; early cost and refinement checks precede
the complete performance gate.

The plan preserves the original appendix's scientific questions: substrate specialization,
differential compatibility, chemical interference, cross-feeding, resistant material and its
degradation, detectable emissions, corpse capture, optional transfer and disturbance. Historical
outcomes remain evidence for their original versions, not required new community configurations.

Current documentation is reconciled when the new implementation lands. This new decision record
and root plan preserve the direction without rewriting accepted records or claiming that planned
laws are active. Existing directories already host the engine and consumer work; no new executable
workspace or compatibility runtime is introduced by planning.

The research basis and full direction are linked from the root plan. Thermodynamic and artificial-
chemistry research inform the abstractions; their real-world or automata-specific implementations
are not imported as additional product requirements.
