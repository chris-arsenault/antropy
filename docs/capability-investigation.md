# Bacterial capability investigation

The current model supports two additional useful evolved capabilities: an observed brain improves
brief-food access with body genes held constant, and an observed physical allele improves funded
B processing and reproduction. Together with the earlier toxin-suppression result, this is evidence
of consequential behavioral and physical evolution. It does not establish stable strategy diversity.

Recommend spatially differentiated A/B resource neighborhoods next, using the existing heterogeneous
deposit capability before designing new physics. The proposal and its falsifier are below; production
configuration and founders remain unchanged.

Subsequent user review clarified the disposition: the investigation establishes useful evolved
differences, but has not established an ecology supporting meaningfully different survival strategies.
Spatial food neighborhoods remain an untested hypothesis, not a selected implementation direction.
The proposed case budget originated with the assistant; exhausting it does not justify leaving the
recommendation unresolved. Future investigation should allocate follow-up capacity around the actual
decision rather than treating a completed panel as sufficient progress toward strategic diversity.

## Staged follow-up rule

Inspect direct sensors/actions, body capacities, capture, exposure, energy/material budgets and
funded divisions before selecting follow-ups. At least the strongest opportunities receive tests
with actual evolved genotypes, selected by a recorded rule independent of their test performance.
Use existing snapshots before any additional selection pilot. Small diagnostic connection changes
measure accessibility, not evolutionary discovery. Preserve negative outcomes and exact conditions.

A benefit that survives a fresh transfer establishes inherited performance for that context.
Targeted reversions or block swaps can locate its cause. Do not infer coexistence, offensive
strategy or useful learned inheritance from abundance, secretion totals or sequence changes alone.

### Rejected recipes and unresolved mechanisms

Reserve braking ends with 5–7 living cells in its ancestry group at the persistent-food horizon when
the fast group is extinct, with tied divisions and lower motor expense (about 21.3% versus 23.7%
of absorbed energy). In brief food it yields no divisions versus 0.875–1 for the fast controller.
The proposed regulation does not solve the access/residence tradeoff; its reserve cue is insufficient
for that recipe. This is not evidence that regulation itself is impossible.

Low motor stock with compensated logits beats the high-motor recipe: persistent-food divisions tie
but final living counts are 16 versus zero; brief-food divisions are one versus zero per founder.
The high-motor cells still swim faster in sampled early trajectories. Logit scaling is not exact
speed matching or an exhaustive search of controller/body coordination. No crossover was found.

High defense in toxin reduces repair expense from about 8.73% to 2.82% of absorbed energy, while
maintenance rises from about 32.67% to 35.53%. Damage approaches zero versus about 0.70% mean
organism-time damage, but divisions remain 0.75 each. Clean-food capture favors reference defense;
toxic-food capture favors high defense. This is a budget tradeoff, not a reproductive defense win.
Uniform imposed exposure has no authored escape gradient, and stationary controllers cannot test
active avoidance. No offensive-producer or avoidance comparison is certified by these cases.

Constant matrix builders spend about 53% of absorbed material on matrix in toxin, and produce zero
divisions versus one per founder for nonbuilders. The conditional controller reads local tonic toxin:
its mean reading falls from 0.0663 at tick 10 to 0.0069 at tick 100, and matrix effort falls from
0.0619 to 0.0065. This connects a changing local cue to reduced secretion.
Toxin-triggered builders spend only about 1.28%,
reduce repair expense to 1.95% versus 7.91%, and tie divisions at 0.75. Both sensor and action are
already available; prolonged secretion is the failed recipe. All cells here are stationary, so
potential drag exposure is not proof that movement drag caused the loss. Binding is a shared field;
separating producer protection from neighbor benefit requires a different spatial control and remains
unresolved. Neither counter totals nor these authored controllers establish evolved construction.

Actual genotype 1783 with baseline recurrence removed captures slightly more B, with tied divisions.
Its private-learning comparison changes the sign of the uptake difference when placement swaps;
so does transfer of the sampled parent's acquired recurrent delta. All give one division per founder.
Active learning spends 0.43–0.44% of absorbed energy in this comparison. Alpha-zero also removes
that expense. Assimilation resets private state and freezes subsequent
learning, but does not erase learning already incorporated into the inherited baseline. The present
modulator directly uses A change, neutral-signal change and energy change; it has no dedicated B or
toxin-change term. That is an interface limitation worth retaining, not proof it caused these negative
results. No larger brain, training campaign or learning redesign follows from this panel.

## Recommendation and smallest falsifier

**Develop simultaneous, spatially differentiated A/B resource neighborhoods next.** Existing local
navigation and physical processing now have actual evolved benefits, and constructed equal-budget
processing has opposing reproductive payoffs. This supplies a causal basis for allowing different
neighborhoods to reward different bodies and movement policies. It does not guarantee coexistence.

Start with the already implemented heterogeneous deposits: omitting `foodEpochs` restores random
A-rich/B-rich compositions. The current epoch setting assigns the same composition to every new
deposit in a phase, reducing the choice between contemporary food types. Review a configuration
direction that retains a declared total A/B input while separating composition in space; add a new
deposit policy only if the existing random heterogeneity cannot provide adequate local contrast.
Do not add a resource species, alter controllers, grant food preferences or promote diagnostic winners.
This is a proposed direction requiring review, not an implemented production change.

The smallest falsifier is four short paired cases: two matched deposits containing the same total
A and B, either both mixed or one A-rich and one B-rich; actual 1847 versus its single-locus
processing reversion, swapped initial assignment, at most 1,500 ticks. Keep inventory, release,
geometry, total funding and initial bodies equal. First inspect the first few hundred ticks of
local A/B contrast, motion and per-type uptake, then divisions. If diffusion erases the contrast
before access, or separation produces no context-dependent processing/growth payoff despite a
readable contrast, the rationale for that spatial setting fails. Stop before another long campaign.
An allocation benefit can arise from local growth and reproduction without evolved active food
choice; report those separately. These proposed cases have not been run after this study's budget.
