# Rugged interaction: ordinary descendants and chemical dependence

**Status:** Local v52 ordinary-world calibration and descendant attribution, October3,2026.
Ordinary births produced large recognition changes and additional chemical use. One matched
descendant assay shows a loss of circuit return offset by additional surrounding chemistry;
this is a conditional dependency, not a demonstrated fitness improvement. Keep lambda3.
No production parameter, founder, controller, live world, publication or deployment changed.

## Decision and interpretation

The [direction](design/rugged-interaction.md) requires surprising inherited consequences and
different relationships to the same chemistry. The previous
[constructed food chain](rugged-community-results.md) established that physical opportunity.
This iteration follows changes born in ordinary production-sized worlds, then separates an
observed binding jump from its other inherited changes. It does not treat a population increase
or a conversion count as the intended ecological result.

Keep the shared steepness at3. Lambda1 increased ordinary growth and survival in this pilot,
but its selected descendant did not obtain an additional-chemistry advantage in the matched
assay. Lambda3 already permits a spontaneous large binding jump and a changed chemical
dependency. These results do not justify globally broadening every founder's recognition.
They also do not establish that3 is optimal, that the selected mutation is adaptive, or that
distinct roles coexist indefinitely.

## Attribution with actual inherited controllers

Register twelve400-tick single-cell cases before stepping: each candidate, its actual parent,
and the candidate with only the largest enzyme bias restored to its parental value; each in
two mixtures. The full mixture is the candidate's native sampled local concentration vector.
The second keeps the same concentrations for0/128/136/8 and omits the additional initial
compounds without redistributing their mass. Subsequent public chemistry can still produce
other identities. Finite uniform doses equal concentration×576 in24×24/mesh2 worlds.

Each starts with the same ordinary founder-zero bound mixture, material1.66, usable work0.5,
zero free inventory, heading0, and reset private memories. Actual inherited genotype proportions
determine body capacities; there are no boosted investments, authored controller weights or
invented routes. Mutation/private learning/inherited learning freeze for attribution. Initial
enzyme0 capacity for the lambda3 descendant and its scalar-restored control is0.0396351; their
entire body vectors agree. Native profiles and action-aware budgets precede stepping, with
snapshot preservation checked. Empty internal inventory makes initial realized processing zero;
import-based analytical ceilings remain positive and cannot predict the later trajectory.

W = living usable work + cumulative paid assembly work − initial usable work. It measures
retained work and funded assembly after expenses; it excludes material potential, gross capture,
overflow and dissipated division work. All twelve cells remain alive at400 ticks. None grows or
divides in these matched fresh-packet conditions, so W here is the change in usable work.
