# Reliability experiment registration

September 14, 2026. Implements phases 4–6 of [the reliability plan](reliability-plan.md).
These are constructed mechanism and operational checks, not evolution certification.

## Inherited machinery access

Question: does complete retirement of a changed slot prevent a nearby inherited
variant from rebuilding and reaching another funded division?

Use seed 101, the existing finite nutrition fixture, ordinary founder chemistry and
a stationary diagnostic RNN. Freeze mutation, private learning and inherited learning.
First allow one parent to obtain its first division through ordinary resource uptake,
processing and construction, within 600 ticks/30 seconds. Save the two funded daughters.
Every arm restores that exact state, resets controller memory equally, and follows both
daughters until the next division, first death, 600 more ticks or 30 seconds.

Six paired comparisons: receptor-0 X +0.12; importer-0 X +0.12; enzyme-0 X +0.12;
enzyme-0 product X offset +1; importer-0 investment locus +0.12; and removal of all
external supply. Each changed arm has an unchanged control. The empty arm isolates
reserve-funded continuation. No body stock or usable energy is granted after birth.

Prediction: target changes immediately retire their slot and temporarily remove its
capacity; investment-only change retains installed stock. A nearby target can rebuild
if uptake from the other machinery and inherited reserves pay its construction. The
empty arm should lose the resource-funded continuation. Read inventory, body stocks,
local readings, injury and cumulative paid flows separately from inherited targets.
If unchanged daughters reproduce and nearby target variants cannot recover, examine
retirement semantics before any tuning. A fixed observation horizon is not a lifetime
fitness comparison. Diagnostic stationary success does not demonstrate expression by
the ordinary moving founder.

## Ordinary habitat access

Use the two actual seed-101 startup source opportunities, translated separately into
the existing 64×64 isolated-source fixture. Preserve each finite source's inventory,
radius, priming and release. Use one ordinary founder with mutation and learning frozen.
For each source compare its resident position, a position eight world units farther
from its center, and no extracellular/source supply. Six cases total, each at most
600 ticks/30 seconds; stop on extinction. Record movement, local readings, uptake,
construction, repair, injury and division. Near-gap failure remains a negative result;
do not manufacture chemotaxis or promote an assay genotype into startup.

## Operational duration

One isolated Chromium session on the existing server; software rendering is explicitly
different from the user's hardware. Freeze physical state at 70 living cells with
100,000 synthetic ancestry records. Repeat production census, selected inspection,
observation publication, WebGL rendering and recovery saves for at most 30 wall minutes.
Track operation timing, bounded retention, WASM allocation and available browser process
memory. Stop early on a fault, invariant violation, unexplained sustained memory growth
or budget breach. Never extend automatically. Separately exercise the actual application
worker/React path and declared 48/2,000/growing capacity workloads.
