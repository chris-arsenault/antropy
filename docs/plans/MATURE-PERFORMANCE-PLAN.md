## Outcome and authority

Reach at least 120 ticks/sec on the existing 180,000-tick, approximately 6,975-cell
reference workload, single threaded. Optimize structural execution first, then shared
numerical bounds and hot loops. Preserve local evolutionary opportunities, funded

same-worker WASM rendering. Matching old trajectories, save compatibility and replay
are not goals. Keep the full physical duration per tick. No population reduction,
hidden mesh reduction, disabled learning or removed observation may manufacture success.

Implementation and bounded performance experiments are authorized. Publication is not
part of this request. Existing staged local-data cleanup belongs to the preceding task.

with panels closed/active, with 71.74 ms average physical stepping in the closed arm.

operators and the ordinary operating benchmark. Preserve frozen donor allocation;
receiver arrivals never finance same-stage outgoing requests. Carry installed support
through gathering and commitment. Reuse spatial search only with actual displacement
and radius-growth validity, including inventory-driven radius changes. Keep current
controller cadence initially. Any later numerical-law change needs its actual mechanism
and opportunity/accounting checks, not identical outputs.

Settled: no multicore, no second physical owner, no recognition-radius shortcut, no
long ecological campaign. Provisional: a shared compact attraction filter may replace
Gaussian offset enumeration after force-curve and symmetry checks. Remaining numerical
choices are implementer-owned and selected from measured dominant costs.
